# Skill — EGregoRA house style (montage)

Drop this into `vendor/OpenMontage/skills/custom/` (the pipeline manifests
allow `custom_skills: true`) and reference it from the edit and compose
stages.

## Register
British spelling. Plain words, no hedging, no disclaimers the viewer did not
ask for. The order addresses adults.

## Cutting
- Hero shots hold longest; cutaways shortest. No two adjacent cuts share both
  subject and scale.
- At most four distinct transition values in a film. Straight cut is the
  default; crossfade only across a change of scale or era.
- Every cut needs a reason written into `edit_decisions`.

## Grading marks
Page markup grades claims with `.sci`, `.hist`, `.spec`, `.myth`. On screen,
say it in words instead — "measured", "recorded", "speculative", "story" — and
never draw the ◆ glyph into a frame.

## Palette and type
Gold `#d7b05a` · bright `#f3ddaa` · dim `#cbbb93` · verdant `#7fae7a` ·
muted `#9f947a` · ground `#0a090e`. Cinzel for display, EB Garamond for body.

## Closing
Every film ends on one line, held four seconds, gold on black, then the
tagline: **Life, Love, Magic.**
