The Eleven Essays

Each of these was written from scratch for this edition: two thousand words or more, in plain language, with the technicalities kept where they earn their place and taken out where they do not. Every essay states what it is claiming, what it is borrowing, and what it owes.

They are meant to be read in order, and each one can be read alone. Number 23 is the place to start if you want the one claim on this site that is arithmetic rather than argument. Number 31 is the place to start if you want to see the framework taking apart its own best claim.

Earned, chosen, refused

Earned. Pn = Fn+2 − 1 and Σ 1/n² = π²/6 are theorems. Hawking radiation, the dephasing of phase relations, and the time-dependence of the 68 : 27 : 5 split are established physics. The Bullet Cluster constraint and the 10−122 discrepancy are real targets, not straw ones.

Chosen. Reading the horizon as a seam; reading the cell cycle as a completed octave; taking 333/666/999 as a scaled image of −1, 0 and 1; the conjecture that where the returners pile is where the numbers crowd; and the reading of the asymmetry as an oscillation rather than a direction. Each is a way of seeing that the evidence permits and does not compel.

Refused. The claim that any of this predicts an experiment it does not predict. The claim that a derivation of a moving target is a derivation. The claim that the arrow comes from Cantor’s inequality. The claim that zero entropy can be waved away. And the flattery of a machine that never says hang on.

What the essays are load-bearing for

These eleven are a coda to the twenty-one chapters, and four of them are doing structural work that the chapters lean on, which is worth saying so that a reader who skips them knows what has been skipped.

Number 23, A Number Made of Itself, is the licence for the whole first part. Without the distinction between a measured constant and a precondition, the claim that π is not found inside the universe is a flourish; with it, it is the premise the chain of necessity hangs from — which is why it is restated in the foundations.

Number 26, The Cell Keeps the Beat, supplies the substrate for the integral self: an integral needs a dx, and the cell cycle is the evidence that biology provides one without being asked. See the beat below the self.

Number 29, Three Numbers Everything Hangs From, is the floor of the building: the fixed points everything else is spliced against. And number 31, Where the Arrow Comes From, carries the correction this edition is built around — the oscillation, and the dephasing mechanism that replaces the withdrawn claim.

The record of changes to these essays, dated, is on the corrections page. The framework’s camera, when it is on, is in the Live Room.

Eleven essays, twenty-six thousand words, and one rule: state the grade of every claim in the same breath as the claim.
