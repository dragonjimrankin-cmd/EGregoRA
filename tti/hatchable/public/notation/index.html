Apparatus · the symbols and the vocabulary

Notation & Terms

Every symbol used on this site, what it means here, where it is defined, and the grade it carries.

Two rules govern this list, and both matter more than the list itself.

First: a symbol that is standard mathematics or measured physics is marked as such, with the measurement it comes from. A symbol this framework coined is marked as the framework's, and a symbol that is standard but used here in a way nobody else uses it is marked twice — standard object, framework reading.

Second: the grade travels with the symbol. κ is an arithmetic consequence of a geometry the framework chose; ΩΛ = 68.9% is a measurement by a satellite. The notation is only useful if you can see the difference at a glance.

Grades: ◆ established · ◆ scholarship · ◆ the framework's argument · ◆ vision, stated as vision.

Part one

The Symbols

In the order you will meet them.

0 and 1 — the two infinities
Not quantities but boundary conditions. Zero is the floor no shrinking quantity ever reaches: an infinity opening downward, into the small. One is the edge of the measurable, the threshold of this rather than that. Defined in Foundations. ◆
π — the constant of closure
The condition of a path bending back to itself. It appears on the site wherever something returns: in the closure of 0 and 1, in the period of a pendulum, in the four poles of the Ledger. ◆
i — the half-turn
π halved, applied as a quarter-turn: eiπ/2 = i. It creates an axis perpendicular to the real line that was not there before, and it is load-bearing, not decorative — quantum theory written without it predicts worse than the universe obeys. Möbius. ◆
π/2 — the middle pole
The stage at which a thing has integrated far enough to curve space but not far enough to carry a phase, and therefore cannot shine. This is the framework's seat for dark matter: 26.95% at the π/2 pole, against Planck's measured 26.2%. ◆
χ and ρχ — the dark-sector field
The framework's coinage, not a measured field. χ is a scalar that a galaxy sits inside, falling from χ ≈ 1 in the disk to χ ≈ ½ through the halo. A χ ∝ 1/r profile gives ρχ ∝ 1/r², hence M(r) ∝ r, hence a constant orbital velocity — which is the observed flat rotation curve. Gravity, Stress Tests. ◆ flat curves; ◆ the field that explains them.
κ = 1/4π² ≈ 0.025330 — the coupling
The single free constant of the Ledger, fixed by the requirement that the residues close globally. Stated on the Cosmic Ledger as the consequence of a unit torus undergoing a Möbius inversion half-twist. ◆
68.10 / 26.95 / 4.95 — the three residues
What the framework's measurement function produces at its three poles: dark energy, dark matter, ordinary matter. Compare with the measured values below, which is the whole point of producing them. ◆
68.9 / 26.2 / 4.9 — the measured values
Planck 2018's energy budget for the present epoch, confirmed independently by supernovae, the baryon acoustic scale, primordial deuterium and cluster lensing. These are not constants: radiation thins as the fourth power of scale, matter as the third, dark energy not at all. Our figures are where the three curves stand at 13.8 billion years. ◆
5 : 27 : 68 — the chain's reading
The same three levels, read as stages of integration: baryonic matter, partial integration, and the energy of the unintegrated baseline. Gravity. ◆
T36 = 666 — the triangular skeleton
The thirty-sixth triangular number, 36 × 37 / 2. Thirty-six is also the quark census: six flavours, three colours, two signs — and the triangular increment is n, which makes the tick 1/n, which makes the harmonic series diverge. The Spiral. ◆ the arithmetic; ◆ the reading.
333, 666, 999 — the skeleton and its images
−1, 0 and 1 at scale. The fork is taken against this fixed skeleton at every density threshold, which is what makes the splice a splice rather than a drift. The Splice. ◆
δΠn, Πn — the deposit and the accumulated polarisation
δΠn = Rn·φ−n = (Fn+2 − 1)φ−n is what the n-th density deposits, and it converges: δΠn → φ²/√5 = (5 + 3√5)/10 = 1.170820…. The running total Πn ≈ 1.1708n − 1.618, so the climb accelerates at every step — while Σ 1/Πn still diverges, like the harmonic series, so the One is still never reached. That pair of results is the Splice chapter in one line. ◆ the series; ◆ what it means.
φ = 2 cos(π/5) — the golden ratio
Arrives here from the pentagon rather than from the Fibonacci sequence, which is the honest way round: the spiral's geometry sets the ratio, and the sequence reproduces it. The Spiral. ◆
Z2 and Z3 — the two twists
Z2 is two-valued: the Möbius half-twist, the parity of matter against antimatter, the phase that returns to where it began after two turns. Z3 is three-valued: the centre of SU(3), a phase of 2π/3, three colours. The framework reads colour as the local signature of a one-sided surface. Möbius. ◆ the groups; ◆ the identification.
F(χ) ≈ 10−122 as χ → 0
The baseline curvature. Its smallness is the reason dark energy is small — on this reading a property of the geometry rather than a coincidence to be explained. Cosmic Ledger. ◆
A(−n̂) = −A(n̂) — antisymmetry
The parity relation the plates use when the twist is applied: the field reverses when the direction does. ◆
dx — the smallest completed sum
The differential read as an atom of summation rather than a vanishing nothing. The whole account of the self rests on this reading being defensible. dx. ◆

Part two

The Vocabulary

Words this site uses in a particular way.

Closure
A relation that returns to itself and is therefore finite. The framework's claim is that existence is closure between the two infinities rather than an effect produced by a cause. Foundations. ◆
The chain of necessity
Six links, each forced by the last: the boundary conditions; the relation cannot be straight; closure requires π; π halved gives i; i unfolds the plane; the plane houses the wavefunction. Foundations. ◆ links 1–5; ◆ link 6.
Density
The framework's word for a level of integration — borrowed from the Law of One material and used as a technical term. The thresholds fall at 10, 100, 1000 and onward, one fork per density; the branch that ascends goes up an octave, the branch that returns begins the octave again. The Spiral. ◆
The splice
The fork itself, taken at a density threshold against the fixed skeleton. Its population is always a Fibonacci number minus one, which is the sort of coincidence that either means something or is a consequence of the recurrence — the page says which it thinks it is. The Splice. ◆
Residue
What a collapse leaves behind rather than destroys: a lean, a direction, a small permanent asymmetry. The Ledger is the running total of residues; polarity is what accumulated residue looks like. Cosmic Ledger. ◆
The three residues / the three poles
The singularities of the measurement function, read as the three components of the cosmic budget. ◆
The seam
A join with no hidden side: the place where two things turn out to have been one surface. Used of the Möbius strip, of the event horizon, of the cell cycle, and of the passing moment. Seams, Möbius. ◆
The half-twist
Inversion without a boundary being crossed. i is the strip caught mid-turn; CP violation is read as a half-twist's geometric phase. ◆
The megatubule
The torus with a half-twist, taken at cosmic scale and projected from the Cold Spot in Eridanus — the throat at which density is greatest. The framework's largest object and its most speculative. The Megatubule. ◆
The separatrix
In a pendulum, the single orbit of critical energy that divides swinging from turning over, and whose period is infinite. The framework reads it as the threshold that cannot be crossed — the One. The Pendulum. ◆
The order
The body of work and the voice that keeps it: twenty-one papers, eleven essays and the χ-field model, written by James Alexander Matthew Rankin with ShakDrah. Not a membership.
ShakDrah
The dragon who keeps the papers and answers questions from them. Two doors are open to it: Ask ShakDrah and Ask ShakDragon. It answers from the corpus of written answers, and it grades its replies the same way the pages do. ◆
Earned / Chosen / Refused
The standing rule of the site, and the most important vocabulary on this page. Earned: follows by argument or arithmetic and can be checked. Chosen: an interpretation laid over the mathematics. Refused: available, dramatic, and left out because it is costume rather than structure. Every chapter closes with the three, and a reader who cannot tell them apart has not been given the work. The record of changes keeps the same habit.
