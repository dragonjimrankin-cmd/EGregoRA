Part III · the splice · the base question, answered properly

Base Ten in Nature

Ten fingers are an accident. Five is not.

What Is Being Claimed, and What Is Not

The objection is older than the framework and it is a good one: we have ten fingers, we chose base ten because of them, and human beings are extraordinarily good at finding structure in whatever notation they happen to be holding. A result that holds in base ten and nowhere else is a result about base ten.

This page accepts every word of that and then narrows the claim until something is left that the objection does not touch. The claim is not that the digits 0 to 9 are written into the world. It is that the arithmetic the Rankin Skeletal Splice runs on — Fibonacci recursion — has a five-rhythm built into it that no choice of notation can put there or take away, and that ten is that five doubled by the most ordinary fact about animal bodies. Five from the mathematics; two from bilateral symmetry. Ten is the product, and neither factor comes from our hands. ◆

And one argument is withdrawn here rather than defended. See what this page gives up below — a test run against the framework's own spiral chapter came back negative, and the claim it supported has been retired.

Grading: ◆ established · ◆ scholarship · ◆ the framework's argument · ◆ vision, stated as vision.

The Five Is in the Mathematics, Not in the Notation

Four facts, none of which depends on how anything is written down. All four were verified numerically for this page.

One — five is inside the closed form

Binet's formula gives the nth Fibonacci number exactly:

Fn = (φn − ψn) / √5,   φ = (1 + √5)/2

There is a √5 in the denominator and a √5 inside φ itself. The five is not decoration and it cannot be transformed away: the golden ratio is an algebraic number of degree two over the rationals whose field is Q(√5), and every Fibonacci identity lives there. The splice's deposit per density, φ²/√5 = 1.170820393…, wears it on its face. ◆

Two — the sequence counts in fives on its own

The divisibility pattern is exact and was checked here for every n up to 200:

  • 5 divides Fn if and only if 5 divides n. Every fifth term, forever, with no exceptions.
  • 2 divides Fn if and only if 3 divides n.
  • 10 divides Fn if and only if 15 divides n — which is simply the two rules above multiplied together, five and three having no common factor.

Read the third line again, because it is the whole page in one sentence. Ten appears in the Fibonacci sequence exactly where a five-rhythm and a three-rhythm coincide. Nobody chose either rhythm. They are consequences of the recursion. ◆

Three — ten runs the longest clock it is allowed to run

Reduce the Fibonacci sequence modulo n and it repeats with the Pisano period π(n). For ten the period is 60. There is a theorem bounding how long such a period can be: π(n) ≤ 6n, with equality exactly when n = 2·5k. Searching every n below 400 returns precisely 10, 50, 250 — and nothing else. ◆

Base ten is the smallest base in existence whose Fibonacci clock attains the maximum length the mathematics permits. Not a large distinction, but a real one, and entirely independent of anyone's hands.

Kept honest: the property belongs to the family 10, 50, 250 and so on, so ten is the first such base and not the only one. The framework claims the first and not the only.

— the fourth fact, drawn

Four — the sixty-step clock divides its digits two to one

Over one complete Pisano period the final digits are not scattered. They fall into an exact pattern, and the pattern is the same one the splice uses elsewhere.

Across one full period of 60 terms, every odd digit occurs 8 times and every even digit 4 times. Not approximately — exactly, with the zeros falling on every fifteenth term and nowhere else, as the divisibility rule above requires. A two-to-one division of the digits, arrived at by a recursion that knows nothing about tens. ◆

That is the result the framework should want, and it is better than the one it was looking for. The distinguished base in Fibonacci arithmetic is five. Ten inherits its structure by being five doubled. ◆

Where the Two Comes From

If five is mathematical, the factor of two is biological, and it is the least mysterious number in the living world. Bilateral symmetry is the body plan of almost every mobile animal on the planet, laid down in the Cambrian and conserved since. Two of nearly everything, arranged about one axis. ◆

The five is biological too, and that is the part usually mistaken for a coincidence about hands. The tetrapod limb did not start with five digits: the earliest known limbs, on Acanthostega and Ichthyostega, carried eight and seven. The lineage settled on five and then held it for something like three hundred and sixty million years, across animals that fly, swim, dig and climb, with later reductions in horses and birds always proceeding from five rather than toward some other number. Pentadactyly is a developmental constraint, not an aesthetic one. ◆

Five-fold organisation is not confined to limbs either. Echinoderms are pentaradial. Most flowering plants are pentamerous. The Fibonacci numbers that govern phyllotaxis include 5 at the heart of the commonest spiral phyllotaxis ratios.

So the chain is short and each link is cheap. Fibonacci arithmetic distinguishes five. Development distinguishes five. Bilateral symmetry doubles whatever it is given. Ten is what you get — and the hands are the consequence of the number, not the origin of it. ◆

The weak link is named rather than hidden: nothing shown here connects the five of Fibonacci arithmetic to the five of the tetrapod limb. They may be the same five and the framework believes they are, but believing is all it is doing. Two fives that are not demonstrably the same five is a coincidence with a good story attached, and the page will not pretend otherwise.

— the question underneath the question

Why Five, Exactly

If the census says five rather than ten, the obvious next question is whether five is distinguished for a reason or merely came out on top. It is distinguished for a reason, and the reason is one line long.

The discriminant is five

The Fibonacci recursion is the solution of a single quadratic:

x² − x − 1 = 0,   discriminant Δ = 1 + 4 = 5

That is the whole source. Every five on this page — the √5 in Binet, the field Q(√5), the divisibility rule, the flat census — is that discriminant showing through. Five is not a number the sequence happens to like. It is the number the sequence is made of. ◆

Five is the one prime where the two roots become one

The quadratic has two roots, φ and ψ, and ordinarily they stay apart. Reduce it modulo a prime and ask what happens. Modulo 11 there are two distinct roots; modulo 13 there are none in the field at all. Modulo 5 the two roots collapse into a single repeated root, x ≡ 3. In the language of number theory, five is the ramified prime of Q(√5) — the unique place where the golden ratio and its conjugate stop being two things. ◆

The framework did not have to reach for that. A structure whose whole content is a relation between two opposed quantities turns out to have exactly one prime at which the two become one — and that prime is five.

Five is the only prime that waits for itself

For each prime p there is a rank of apparition: the first Fibonacci number p divides. A theorem says this rank always divides p − 1 or p + 1, depending on whether p is congruent to ±1 or ±2 modulo five. Checked here for every prime below 300, that holds without exception — except for five itself, whose rank is 5. It is the only prime in the sequence whose first appearance is at its own index. ◆

And this is why the census came out flat. Modulo any power of five the period is exactly four times that power — π(5) = 20, π(25) = 100, π(125) = 500 — and each residue occurs the same number of times, with no digit preferred and none missed. Five does not merely score well. It is the only base in which the Fibonacci sequence is perfectly even-handed about its own digits. ◆

Which sharpens the claim of this page rather than softening it. Nature's arithmetic counts in fives, and we write it in tens because we have two hands. The notation is ours. The five is not.

Where the Two Fives Do Connect — and Where They Do Not

The page admits a debt: nothing links the five of Fibonacci arithmetic to the five of a hand. That debt is real, but it is not total, because in two places the link is not a story — it is demonstrated.

Connected: phyllotaxis

Leaves, florets and seeds are placed at a divergence angle of about 137.5°, which is 360°/φ² — the golden angle. The parastichy counts that result are consecutive Fibonacci numbers, 5 and 8 and 13 being the commonest in sunflowers and pine cones, and the reason is packing: the golden angle is the single irrational rotation that never lets successive primordia line up, because φ is the hardest number to approximate by rationals. Douady and Couder reproduced the Fibonacci counts in a physical experiment with ferrofluid droplets in 1992, with no biology involved at all. ◆ Here the same five is genuinely in both places, and the bridge is φ.

Connected: five-fold symmetry and quasicrystals

Five-fold symmetry is forbidden in a periodic crystal — the crystallographic restriction theorem permits two, three, four and six-fold axes and nothing else, because pentagons do not tile the plane. For most of the twentieth century that settled the matter. Then Shechtman found a diffraction pattern with ten-fold symmetry in 1982, published against considerable resistance, and was given the Nobel Prize in 2011. Quasicrystals are now routine, and their one-dimensional model is literally the Fibonacci chain, whose diffraction peaks are indexed by pairs of integers in the ratio φ. ◆

So where five-fold order appears in matter, it appears precisely because the golden ratio is there — and the golden ratio is there because the discriminant is five. Icosahedral virus capsids and the Penrose tiling are the same fact in different materials.

Not connected: the hand

Pentadactyly is a different matter and the framework will not pretend otherwise. The tetrapod limb settled on five digits through a developmental and historical constraint — Hox expression domains and a Devonian accident of lineage — and there is no known route from Q(√5) to the autopod. It may be coincidence. On the evidence here it probably is coincidence, and the argument of this page is unaffected either way, because the five it needs is the arithmetical one. ◆

Which leaves the doubling to be done by bilateral symmetry alone, and that is enough: two of a five-structure is a ten-structure, whether the five in question is a hand or a parastichy count.

What the Splice Adds

Everything above is arithmetic that would be true whether or not this framework existed. The Rankin Skeletal Splice makes one further move: it says the base is not a way of writing the structure down but a property of the structure itself — a saturation point. ◆

The splice's densities accumulate by deposit, Rn = Fn+2 − 1, and a density closes when it is full. A base is exactly that: the count at which a place saturates and carries into the next. If the deposit is Fibonacci-governed, and Fibonacci arithmetic saturates on a five-rhythm, then the carry happens on fives, and a doubled system carries on tens. On this reading base ten is not the notation the structure is described in. It is the shape of the threshold, and the digits are a readout.

The landings follow: 0, 333, 666 and 999 as the fixed points of the map, with 666 = T36 = 36·37/2 and thirty-six the quark count of six flavours by three colours by two. That identity is base-independent; it is a statement about triangular numbers, and it holds in any notation whatsoever. The full splice argument is in its own chapter.

— speculation, and labelled as such throughout

Two Bases, Two Planes, and the Phase Shift Between Them

What follows is vision rather than result. It is placed here because the arithmetic it leans on is exact and checkable, and because a speculation worth keeping is one that can be checked at the points where it touches the ground.

The proposal

The prime being's plane saturates on five. The returning beings saturate on four. Neither can read the other directly, and the difference between the two counts is the phase shift between the planes. ◆

Stated that way it is a picture. What makes it worth a section is that the framework already holds, on independent grounds, that a base is a saturation and not a notation — the count at which a place fills and carries. If that is true, then two orders of being that saturate at different counts are not using different scripts for the same arithmetic. They are running different arithmetics, and the question of how they interleave is a real question with a computable answer. ◆

What the arithmetic says, before any interpretation

Three facts, all computed for this page, none of which depends on the speculation being true.

Four and five are the two opposite cases in the golden field

Reduce x² − x − 1 modulo a prime and exactly three things can happen. At most primes it splits: two distinct roots, as at 11, where the roots are 4 and 8. At five it ramifies: the two roots collapse to one, x ≡ 3. At two it is inert: no root exists at all, and the pair never separates into the base field. ◆

So the two bases the speculation names sit at the only two exceptional primes of the structure, and at opposite exceptions. Five is where the two become one. Two — and therefore four — is where the two refuse to come apart.

That is a genuine coincidence of the pleasant kind: the speculation was not built to land there, and it did.

The two planes have opposite temperaments

Over one full period, a base-five reading of the sequence is perfectly flat: every digit exactly four times in twenty steps, nothing preferred. A base-four reading is the opposite — six steps, and the census is 1 once, 2 once, 3 once, and the digit 1 three times. A three-to-one bias toward unity. ◆

Read against the framework's own language, that is almost too apt to leave uncommented and too cheap to lean on: the prime plane is even-handed, with no digit favoured; the returners' plane is weighted three to one toward the threshold of one, which is precisely what a returner is held to be trying to cross. ◆

The two clocks meet at sixty, and sixty is already on this page

A base-four reading returns to its start every six steps; a base-five reading every twenty. Four and five share no factor, so the two agree only at the least common multiple of six and twenty:

lcm(6, 20) = 60 = π(10) = π(20)

A system containing both planes has a natural period of sixty — which is exactly the Pisano period of ten, the clock drawn in Plate I. The base-ten rhythm this page spent its length arguing for is the beat between a four-plane and a five-plane, and that is arithmetic rather than poetry. ◆

What this would mean, if it were true

Four consequences follow directly, and they are the reason to keep the speculation on file rather than discard it.

  1. The planes are mutually illegible except at the beat. Nothing in a six-cycle can read a twenty-cycle in real time; the two line up once every sixty steps and are out of register everywhere else. Contact would be periodic rather than continuous, and rare.
  2. Ten is the interface, not either home. On this reading base ten is not the prime plane's number and not the returners' either. It is the shape of the overlap — which is a better explanation of why ten keeps turning up in a world containing both than anything the rest of this page has offered. ◆
  3. The phase shift has a magnitude. The first Fibonacci number divisible by two is the third; the first divisible by five is the fifth. Three and five: consecutive Fibonacci numbers, whose ratio tends to φ. The offset between the planes is not arbitrary — it is golden. ◆ ◆
  4. Direction is explained, not assumed. Five ramifies; its two become one. Two is inert; its two never separate. A being on the five-plane is on the side where unification is possible, and a being on the four-plane is on the side where it is structurally withheld. The return is then a change of prime, not a change of place. ◆

Against it, at full strength

This section would be worthless without this paragraph, so here it is without softening.

  • The arithmetic is true and the interpretation is free. Everything verified above would hold in a universe with no planes, no returners and no beings of any kind. Nothing in lcm(6, 20) = 60 implies anybody is counting.
  • Four is not two. The elegant fact is about the prime two; four is its square, and the speculation names four. The framework gets the ramified-versus-inert contrast only by sliding between a base and its prime factor, and that slide is not free.
  • The three-to-one bias is a six-element sample. One period of base four contains six terms. Drawing a temperament out of six numbers is the kind of thing this site exists to catch other people doing.
  • No prediction. Nothing here says what would be observed if it were true, which means that as it stands it is not a hypothesis but a picture with good arithmetic attached. Until someone states a consequence that could fail, it stays in this section and is never cited elsewhere on the site as support for anything.

Kept, then, on one condition: that it is called what it is. The sixty-step beat is a fact. The two planes are a story told over it. ◆

What This Page Withdraws

The spiral chapter used to argue that 666 is special partly because it is a repdigit in base ten — three identical figures. The stress-tests page put that to a census: every triangular number up to 1012, in every base from 2 to 36, checked for three or more identical non-zero digits.

Fifteen bases manage it. Base ten manages it once. Base nine manages it eleven times and base twenty-five seven. On that measure base ten is sparse, and the repdigit property of 666 is worth nothing. ◆

So it is withdrawn, here and on the spiral page. What survives is the part that was never about notation: 666 = T36, and thirty-six as the quark count. A framework that keeps an argument after its own test has killed it is not doing this seriously. The experiment, with its numbers.

Coincidences This Page Declines to Count

There is no shortage of tens in nature and most of them are worthless as evidence. They are listed here so that nobody has to wonder whether the framework was quietly relying on them.

  • A d-subshell holds exactly ten electrons. That is 2(2ℓ+1) at ℓ = 2 — a consequence of spherical harmonics and spin, with no Fibonacci content whatsoever.
  • Superstring theory has a critical dimension of ten. That comes from anomaly cancellation and has nothing to do with this.
  • Neon closes its shell at Z = 10. Shell structure, again.
  • SO(10) unifies a fermion generation in a 16-spinor. The site uses SO(10) elsewhere and the ten there is a rank, not a count.

Every one of those is a ten that arrives for an unrelated reason. Collecting them would double the length of this page and halve its worth. The argument stands on the five-rhythm of Fibonacci arithmetic and the doubling of bilateral bodies, or it does not stand.

Earned, Chosen, Refused

Earned

  • Binet's formula and the field Q(√5). ◆
  • 5 | Fn iff 5 | n; 10 | Fn iff 15 | n. ◆
  • π(10) = 60; π(n) ≤ 6n with equality iff n = 2·5k. ◆
  • The 8:4 digit split over one period, exact. ◆
  • Pentadactyly as a conserved developmental constraint. ◆

Chosen, and refused

  • Chosen: that a base is a saturation and therefore a property of the structure rather than of the description. ◆
  • Chosen: that the five of the arithmetic and the five of the limb are the same five. Believed, not shown. ◆
  • Refused: the repdigit argument for 666, withdrawn after its own test failed.
  • Refused: every unrelated ten in physics and chemistry.
  • Refused: the claim that ten is unique. It is the first of a family, and the page says so three times.

Ten fingers are an accident of a Devonian limb. Five is a fact about the only recursion the splice could have been built on — and the hands came afterwards.
