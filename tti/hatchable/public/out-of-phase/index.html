A speculative essay · matter, antimatter, and the unfinished cancellation

The Residue That Did Not Meet Its Opposite

Perhaps matter did not defeat antimatter. Perhaps both were residues of an earlier interaction, deposited into different modes of the same geometry, and what survived was simply what could no longer reach its complement.

The proposal in one sentence

Matter as Residue

The visible universe may be made not from the substance that won, but from the tiny remainder that failed to cancel.

Write the early populations as one large matched part and one small difference:

\[ n_B=n_0+\delta, \qquad n_{\bar B}=n_0. \]

Pair annihilation removes the common term \(n_0\). What remains is the signed residue

\[ n_B-n_{\bar B}=\delta, \qquad \eta_B=\frac{n_B-n_{\bar B}}{n_\gamma}\approx6\times10^{-10}. \]

The broad physical picture is established: the observable cosmos contains roughly one surviving baryon per billion photons, and ordinary matter–antimatter annihilation removes matched pairs rather than changing their signed difference. ◆ What is not established is the interpretation offered here: that the missing complement may still exist in another phase sector and that the residue records an interaction earlier than the cancellation we can reconstruct. ◆

Grading on this page: ◆ established physics or mathematics · ◆ a conditional mechanism · ◆ the framework's cosmological reading.

✦

A reversible warning

What the Baker’s Map Does — and Does Not — Give Us

Begin with twenty thousand points gathered into one small blob. Put them through an area-preserving, reversible baker’s map and measure their Shannon entropy on a fixed grid. In the numerical run that prompted this essay, the coarse-grained entropy rose by 8.323 bits forwards and 8.321 bits backwards. The asymmetry was only 0.002 bits.

The map has no preferred temporal direction. It stretches and folds the blob into filaments whichever way its clock is run. Once those filaments become thinner than the grid, the observer counts a spread rather than a pattern. Fine-grained information remains; coarse-grained access to it does not. ◆

\[ S_{\mathrm{fine}}(t)=S_{\mathrm{fine}}(0), \qquad S_{\mathrm{grid}}(\pm t)>S_{\mathrm{grid}}(0). \]

The almost equal increases say that the arrow lies in the specially prepared blob and in the grid through which it is viewed, not in the reversible map. The small numerical difference is consistent with finite sampling and binning; it is not a dynamical arrow.

This matters because it demonstrates a possibility often lost in the language of beginnings: a macroscopic asymmetry can be the visible history of a boundary condition under symmetric rules. But it also sets a limit. Entropy is an unsigned measure of inaccessible detail. Baryon number is signed. A reversible mixer can hide where the red and blue points went; if it preserves their colours, it cannot turn equal numbers of them into a net excess.

The baker’s map can explain how a residue becomes unreadable as an interaction. It cannot, by itself, explain why the residue carries the sign of matter.

The earlier event

A Cancellation Can Preserve a Difference; It Cannot Create One

Suppose an earlier interaction produced a complementary pair:

\[ X\longrightarrow M_A+\bar M_B, \qquad B_A+B_B=0. \]

An observer confined to sector \(A\) measures \(B_A>0\). An observer confined to \(B\) measures the opposite. The complete state remains balanced even though neither local history looks balanced. On this reading, the question is no longer why matter defeated antimatter. It is why the interaction correlated the sign of baryon number with the sector into which each residue was deposited.

There are several places such an event could be imagined: an early symmetry-breaking transition, a bounce from an earlier cosmological phase, a collision between branches, or the passage through the framework’s Möbius seam. Those are not equivalent hypotheses, and none has evidence supplied by the geometry alone. ◆

Nor does annihilation solve the sorting problem. The reaction

\[ B+\bar B\longrightarrow\text{radiation} \]

removes one from each side and therefore preserves \(B-\bar B\). A non-zero global residue requires baryon-number-changing physics. A globally balanced but locally divided residue requires a separation mechanism. The framework may choose either debt, but it cannot decline both.

What phase must mean

An Angle Is Not Yet a Wall

A global complex phase cannot protect antimatter. If

\[ |\bar\psi\rangle=e^{i\phi}|\psi\rangle, \]

then the magnitude of the overlap is unchanged:

\[ \left|\langle\psi|\bar\psi\rangle\right|^2 =\left|e^{i\phi}\right|^2=1. \]

Ordinary matter and antimatter still annihilate when their states meet. For “out of phase” to do physical work, phase must label different internal modes, topological sheets, vacuum sectors or values of an additional coordinate. The annihilation rate can then be written conditionally as

\[ \Gamma_{\mathrm{ann}} =n_Mn_{\bar M}\langle\sigma v\rangle\, \mathcal O(\Delta\theta), \qquad 0\leq\mathcal O\leq1, \]

where \(\mathcal O\) is a real overlap or selection factor. The three possibilities are now precise: \(\mathcal O\) can vanish permanently, open periodically, or return arbitrarily close to an opening without ever exactly repeating.

✦

Possibility one

Permanent Orthogonality

The two residues occupy modes that no ordinary annihilation operator can join.

The mechanism

\[ \langle\chi_M|\chi_{\bar M}\rangle=0, \qquad \mathcal O=0. \]

The sector label is conserved. Matter and complementary antimatter can coexist in the complete geometry without sharing an electromagnetic or strong-interaction channel. Their separation is not distance but orthogonality.

What it buys

This is the cleanest protection against annihilation. It permits exact global balance, explains the absence of an annihilation boundary in our sky, and does not depend on catching the phases at a fortunate moment.

What might cross

If both sectors contribute stress-energy to one metric, then

\[ G_{\mu\nu}=8\pi G \left(T^{A}_{\mu\nu}+T^{B}_{\mu\nu}\right). \]

The complement could gravitate while remaining dark to ordinary light. It would resemble a hidden or mirror sector, not laboratory antimatter placed behind an invisible screen.

The debt

The theory must derive the conserved label, the forbidden interaction matrix element and the shared gravitational coupling. Without those, “another plane” only renames the absence of annihilation. ◆

Possibility two

Periodic Overlap

The channel is usually closed, but the clocks repeatedly bring the residues back into contact.

Five against eight

The forty-tick window

\[ \Delta\theta_{5\to8}(n)=\frac{3\pi n}{20}, \qquad N_{5,8}=40. \]

The return plane lags by \(27^\circ\) per shared tick, reaches opposition after twenty and returns to alignment after forty. If coupling follows alignment, annihilation or exchange would occur in a recurring forty-tick window.

Five against four

The twenty-tick window

\[ \Delta\theta_{5\to4}(n)=-\frac{\pi n}{10}, \qquad N_{5,4}=20. \]

The return plane leads by \(18^\circ\) per tick, reaches opposition after ten and alignment after twenty. The sign changes the handedness of the approach; the shorter period doubles the frequency of possible contact.

The cost of opening the channel

A gentle modulation is not enough. For example,

\[ \mathcal O(\Delta\theta) =\cos^2\!\left(\frac{\Delta\theta}{2}\right) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \overline{\mathcal O}=\frac12. \]

That would halve the long-term rate, not suppress it. In the dense early universe even a brief open channel could erase the residues. Survival instead depends on the integrated optical depth,

\[ P_{\mathrm{survive}} =\exp\!\left[-\int\Gamma_{\mathrm{ann}}(t)\,dt\right], \]

so the overlap windows must be extremely narrow, the cross-sector coupling extremely weak, or the first effective reopening delayed until cosmic expansion has reduced the density. The reward for that difficulty is falsifiability: periodic coupling invites searches for bursts, oscillations, conversion events or energy injection tied to one recurrence time. The framework does not yet identify the physical duration of a tick. ◆

Possibility three

Quasiperiodic Separation

The scales never exactly rephase, but physical interactions need more than exact arithmetic non-coincidence to remain closed.

If the phase follows multiplicative density scales rather than finite clocks, write

\[ \Phi_b(n)=2\pi\left\{n\log_b5\right\}, \qquad b\in\{4,8\}. \]

Both logarithms are irrational. Exact positive-integer alignment would require \(5^n=b^m\), which is impossible because powers of five and powers of two have no common prime factors. The phase never repeats exactly. ◆

Yet the sequence is dense around the circle. For an interaction window with fractional-cycle width \(\varepsilon\), contact occurs whenever

\[ \left\|n\log_b5\right\|\lt\varepsilon. \]

Near-alignments then occur infinitely often. Over a long run, roughly a fraction \(2\varepsilon\) of the ticks lies inside the window, giving a characteristic wait of order

\[ N_{\mathrm{encounter}}\sim\frac{1}{2\varepsilon}. \]

The events would be deterministic but non-repeating: not a forty-tick pulse, but irregular leakage. This may produce a very long-lived residue if the portal is narrow. It does not produce absolute protection. “Never exactly equal” is a mathematical statement; every physical interaction has a linewidth. ◆

The first best explains survival. The second is the most sharply testable. The third is the most mathematically subtle, but exact incommensurability alone is not a physical shield.

The conventional test the speculation must still pass

Separation Is Not Baryogenesis

For a universe beginning with zero net baryon number to end with a genuine global excess, conventional baryogenesis requires the Sakharov conditions: baryon-number violation, C and CP violation, and a departure from thermal equilibrium. ◆ These allow reactions to create a bias and cosmic expansion to prevent the inverse reactions from erasing it.

The phase-sector proposal takes a different route only if the global excess remains zero:

\[ B_{\mathrm{visible}}=-B_{\mathrm{complement}}, \qquad B_{\mathrm{total}}=0. \]

It can then replace global baryogenesis with local sorting. But that does not make the problem vanish. The earlier interaction must still correlate matter with one sector and antimatter with the other. If the sorting is exactly symmetric, our matter branch is one member of a complementary pair. If one sign is dynamically preferred, CP violation has returned through the side door.

Nor should the complementary sector casually be called ordinary antimatter. If it cannot share our strong and electromagnetic vertices, it is a hidden CPT-related sector. Its contents may be conjugate to ours while its phenomenology resembles mirror or dark matter. The name must follow the coupling, not the poetry.

What the idea volunteers for examination

Five Debts That Can Become Tests

One — define the sector

Name the observable or quantum number that makes the two states orthogonal. A plane in a picture is not yet a degree of freedom in a theory.

Two — write the portal

Specify the operator that permits gravity, mixing or occasional conversion while suppressing strong and electromagnetic annihilation. Its coupling must be calculable.

Three — survive the hot universe

Integrate the annihilation rate through the high-density era and show that the proposed residue is not erased before nucleosynthesis or excessively injected into radiation.

Four — count the hidden energy

A gravitating complementary sector affects expansion, structure, lensing and the radiation budget. Those effects must fit the cosmic microwave background and light-element abundances.

Five — identify the clock

Periodic and quasiperiodic models require a physical tick, a linewidth and a map from phase to interaction strength. Without them, recurrence counts geometry rather than events.

The clean failure

If the sectors have ordinary overlap, if the hidden abundance violates cosmological limits, or if no interaction can sort baryon sign by sector, the proposal fails rather than retreating into an unobservable plane.

A synthesis, not a fourth escape

A Weak Phase-Dependent Portal

The most durable version combines approximate orthogonality with a small, phase-dependent opening:

\[ \Gamma_{\mathrm{ann}} =\Gamma_0\,\epsilon^2F(\Delta\theta), \qquad \epsilon\ll1. \]

The factor \(\epsilon^2\) provides survival. The function \(F\) supplies either periodic windows or quasiperiodic leakage. This avoids demanding that a decimal inequality perform the work of a symmetry, while preserving an observational edge: a hidden complement is almost inaccessible, not inaccessible by definition.

It is also the version most faithful to the residue picture. The previous interaction need not be recoverable in detail. Its unmatched products can persist because the channel that once joined them has nearly closed. What remains is not evidence of victory. It is evidence of incomplete contact. ◆

Earned, Chosen, Refused

Earned
Pair annihilation preserves the signed baryon difference. A global phase alone does not change an overlap probability. Orthogonal modes can forbid a matrix element. The cyclic five-to-eight and five-to-four clocks close after forty and twenty ticks respectively. Irrational logarithmic phases never exactly repeat but return arbitrarily close. Reversible fine-grained dynamics can display a two-sided rise of coarse-grained entropy.
Chosen
That visible matter is the residue of an earlier interaction; that complementary antimatter was sorted into another sector; that both sectors may source one gravitational field; and that their interaction is permanently, periodically or quasiperiodically suppressed.
Refused
Calling an ordinary phase factor a barrier. Claiming that annihilation creates a signed excess. Treating exact arithmetic non-alignment as zero physical coupling. Calling hidden matter antimatter without specifying the conjugation. Using an unseen plane to evade every possible test.

Closing stance

Matter may be not what defeated antimatter, but what remained unmatched when an earlier interaction divided, paired and cancelled the two.

If the complement survives, its absence from our instruments must be caused by a structure precise enough to calculate: a conserved orthogonality, a narrow recurring window, or a quasiperiodic portal with a finite rate. Until that structure exists, the thought is a disciplined possibility rather than a physical theory.
