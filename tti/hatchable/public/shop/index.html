How an order works

  1. Press Order on the thing you want. The order slip opens; the gate-word keeps the bots out.
  2. Pay by card there and then, or send the order to be followed up by hand. Either way it is written to the ledger the moment it is made, and an email goes to the order.
  3. Card details go to Stripe, never to this site. A confirmation follows either way, with a first word on timing.
  4. The goods are made or gathered, and go in the post. Tracking comes by email.

Returns: unworn, within thirty days, to shakradragon@gmail.com with the order reference. The fox and the badger are, as ever, non-refundable in principle.
