A TTI conjecture · what arrives from before zero

JWST’s Little Red Dots, Through TTI

Suppose the dots are the youngest things we can see because they are also the oldest things still arriving: residues of a previous universe, carried through the hidden interval between minus one and zero and projected into ours as compact red seams.

The stronger speculation in one sentence

An Integration Front Carrying an Older Residue

A little red dot may be the positive-side appearance of something that did not begin in this universe: a prior cosmic residue returning from \(-1\), moving through \((-1,0)\), and crossing the zero seam as a seed around which the new universe rapidly integrates.

On the positive side, the event would still look like the local process already proposed here: a young galactic nucleus climbing from the dark-matter-like middle state \(\chi\simeq\tfrac12\) toward luminous completion at \(\chi\simeq1\). The new thought is that the climb is triggered or pre-patterned by an inherited negative-side residue rather than beginning from featureless initial conditions.

The black hole would be the closure knot where the old residue acquires a new physical body. The dense red source around it would be the seam’s visible aftermath: ordinary gas heated and reprocessed by an object whose boundary condition is older than its host galaxy. The dot is not the previous galaxy surviving intact. It is what that galaxy, universe, or global residue leaves when almost everything except its capacity to seed structure has been stripped away. ◆

Grading on this page: ◆ the minimal observational anchor · ◆ a conditional TTI mechanism · ◆ philosophical image only. Neither a previous universe nor a physical \((-1,0)\) sector is established by astronomy. Both are new extensions of the framework.

The sky, kept brief

What the Conjecture Is Trying to Explain

Webb has found an abundant early population of extremely compact sources with blue rest-ultraviolet light, steep red rest-optical continua and, in many examples, spectral evidence for accreting massive black holes. They become common in the first billion years and decline rapidly later. Dense gas around young black holes is presently the leading physical account of their unusual spectra. ◆

TTI does not replace that radiative-transfer account. It places a speculative geometric process beneath it: the cocoon is the material expression of an integration boundary, and the rapidly growing black hole is the centre around which the boundary closes. The observed redness still comes from atoms, gas, scattering, absorption and cosmological redshift. There is no special “red frequency” in the \(\chi\)-field or the negative interval.

The interval the framework has not yet inhabited

What Could Exist Between Minus One and Zero?

The existing Cosmic Ledger uses \(R_{\infty}=-1\) as a residue at infinity: the balancing term required when the three positive residues sum to one. In mathematics, that minus sign is bookkeeping, not a hidden country. The interval \((-1,0)\) is simply another real interval, with the same cardinality as \((0,1)\). Negative numbers are not automatically earlier times or previous worlds. ◆

The new conjecture deliberately takes one step further. It treats \(-1\) not only as the closing residue but as the orientation-reversed image of completed \(+1\), and treats the open interval between \(-1\) and zero as a residue corridor: what remains after one universe has closed but before its surviving pattern has been given positive measure in another.

\[ \mathcal U_{n-1} \xrightarrow{\;\text{closure}\;} R_{\infty}^{(n-1)}=-1 \longrightarrow \mathcal N=(-1,0) \xrightarrow{\;0^-\sim0^+\;} \mathcal U_n=(0,1). \]

Zero is not crossed as an ordinary location. The two approaches \(0^-\) and \(0^+\) are identified as opposite faces of one seam, in the same spirit as the framework’s Möbius return. The negative side carries orientation and residue; the positive side supplies measurable matter and a new causal history. ◆

Reading A — literal ancestry

A remnant of a previous universe

A prior universe reaches closure, loses almost all local structure, and leaves a compressed residue at \(-1\). What crosses the zero seam is not an old galaxy with its stars intact but a seed condition: mass, topology, phase memory, information, or some combination still to be specified. A little red dot is the first luminous body assembled around one emergence point in the new universe.

Reading B — structural coexistence

A window into the \((-1,0)\) sector

No earlier universe is required. The negative interval can be a simultaneously existing, orientation-reversed sector of this universe. A little red dot then marks a local intersection where that hidden sector projects into positive measure. “Previous” describes its order in the closure map, not an earlier date on our cosmic clock.

One residue, many red dots

The Multiplicity Need Not Create Many Prime Sources

If the previous universe has one globally transmissible Prime residue, hundreds of little red dots cannot each be a separate Prime Being arriving intact. The coherent version is one-to-many: one global negative-side boundary condition is sampled at many local emergence points, just as one field can seed many condensations.

\[ R_{-}^{(n-1)} \xrightarrow{\;\mathcal K_{-+}\;} \bigl\{s_1^{(n)},s_2^{(n)},\ldots,s_N^{(n)}\bigr\}, \qquad \text{one source residue, many local seeds}. \]

The dots would share an ancestry without sharing an identity. They are not fragments of a person and do not multiply the Prime role. They are local physical consequences of one inherited global condition. This keeps the one-Prime-per-universe rule intact while allowing the sky to contain many punctures. ◆

A proposed crossing map and a local χ-front

Where the Dot Would Live Mathematically

Introduce \(\xi\in(-1,0)\) as a residue coordinate, not an extra spatial direction that a telescope could point toward. Let \(r_-(\xi,y)\) describe whatever pattern survives on that branch. A seam kernel \(\mathcal K_{-+}\) would project it into source events in positive spacetime:

\[ J_+(x,t)=g\!\int_{-1}^{0}\!d\xi\int d\mu_-(y)\, \mathcal K_{-+}(x,t;\xi,y)\,r_-(\xi,y). \]

This equation names the missing mechanism. The kernel must say where a residue can emerge, whether orientation is reversed, how locality is recovered, and why the events cluster in the young universe. Its coupling \(g\), measure \(d\mu_-\), dimensions and dynamics are all presently unknown.

The inherited source could then enter a positive-side evolution law rather than replacing it:

\[ \frac{D\chi}{Dt}=F_{\rm TTI}[\chi,g_{\mu\nu},\rho,T]+J_+(x,t). \]

Define the observable little-red-dot seam as the region where the local field is both intermediate and changing rapidly:

\[ \Sigma_{\rm LRD}(t)= \left\{x:\frac12<\chi(x,t)<1, \quad \frac{D\chi}{Dt}>0, \quad |\nabla\chi|>\chi_{\rm c}\right\}. \]

The first condition places the region between the Ledger’s middle and final states. The second says it is becoming more integrated rather than relaxing back toward the dark state. The third makes it a narrow front rather than an entire galaxy glowing uniformly.

A phenomenological luminosity for the transition could then be written

\[ L_{\chi}=\epsilon_{\chi} \int_{\Sigma_{\rm LRD}} \rho c^2\,\max\!\left(\frac{D\chi}{Dt},0\right)dV, \]

where \(\epsilon_{\chi}\) is the fraction of integration energy reaching radiation. The observed spectrum would still be this power plus ordinary accretion light after gas has absorbed, scattered and re-emitted it:

\[ L_{\lambda}^{\rm obs} =\mathcal T_{\lambda}(\rho,T,\tau) \left[L_{\lambda}^{\rm acc}+L_{\lambda}^{\chi}\right]. \]

The conservation fork

If only information or topology crosses, \(J_+\) selects initial conditions but contributes no energy; the black hole and cocoon must be paid for entirely by matter already in this universe. If mass-energy crosses, the two branches require a balanced exchange current:

\[ \nabla_{\mu}T_{+}^{\mu\nu}=J_{-+}^{\nu}, \qquad \nabla_{\mu}T_{-}^{\mu\nu}=-J_{-+}^{\nu}, \qquad \Delta E_+ + \Delta E_-=0. \]

The second option risks double counting: the inherited energy cannot also be ordinary accretion energy, and the negative label does not licence negative mass. A third, weaker possibility is that no substance crosses at all; the two sides merely satisfy one global junction condition. These alternatives lead to different observations and cannot be blended when convenient.

These equations make the idea writable, not true.

Neither \(\mathcal K_{-+}\), \(g\), \(\epsilon_{\chi}\), the threshold \(\chi_{\rm c}\), nor the evolution law for \(\chi\) has been derived from TTI’s modified Einstein equations. If they can be tuned separately for every object, the proposal explains nothing. The debt is to derive them once and then let the sky refuse the result. ◆

Four parts of TTI meet at the dot

Inheritance, Integration, Seam, and Torus

Inheritance

The \((-1,0)\) corridor supplies a proposed antecedent. It says why an exceptionally compact seed might already possess closure structure at cosmic dawn. It does not yet say whether the inheritance is energy, information or a junction condition. ◆

Integration

The \(\chi\)-field supplies the positive-side move from partial to complete phase. The old residue, if real, initiates or biases that move; it does not replace the gas, gravity and local geometry that perform it. ◆

Seam

The event horizon is TTI’s stellar-scale closure boundary. The stronger conjecture nests one seam inside another: a black-hole horizon on the positive side localises the deeper identification \(0^-\sim0^+\). ◆

Torus

Accreting material circulates while fields and outflows return through a larger loop. TTI reads that as its toroidal grammar, but ordinary accretion theory already explains the circulation. Shape alone is not evidence for inherited residue. ◆

Why early, why compact, why temporary?

The New Universe Was Receiving and Building

TTI says dark matter is not inert material waiting beside ordinary matter. It is partial integration: the gravitational scaffold that makes complete integration possible. The inheritance conjecture adds that this scaffold may not be born wholly blank. In the first billion years, its steepest gradients may mark where negative-side boundary data are being resolved into positive structure.

The full conjectured order is:

\[ \underbrace{r_-(\xi)}_{-1<\xi<0} \xrightarrow{\;\mathcal K_{-+}\;} \underbrace{\chi\simeq\tfrac12}_{\text{dark scaffold}} \longrightarrow \underbrace{\Sigma_{\rm LRD}}_{\text{compact seam}} \longrightarrow \underbrace{\chi\simeq1}_{\text{luminous host}}. \]

Early: inherited seeds would emerge most strongly near the new universe’s initial seam. Compact: the residue would resolve first at the deepest closure centre, around the young black hole, before integration fills the host. Temporary: once the inherited condition is spent and the outer region reaches the completed phase, luminosity is distributed through a galaxy rather than trapped in one red point.

This gives a sharper TTI reading of an apparently overmassive black hole in a faint host. The centre is not merely completing before its surroundings; it may begin with an inherited closure advantage. That possibility must predict more than rapid seed formation already can, or it adds only a story. ◆

What would turn the picture into an explanation?

Six Debts the Sky Can Collect

One crossing law

Derive both \(\mathcal K_{-+}\) and \(D\chi/Dt\) from one action rather than choosing them object by object. The same law must fix where residues emerge and how long their luminous phase lasts.

An inheritance clock

The supply from the zero seam must decay at a calculable rate. It should predict the observed early abundance and later decline, together with a source-to-host sequence as each luminous galaxy catches up with its compact inherited seed.

A shared-residue signature

One global residue sampled many times should impose a population-level covariance: a constrained relation among seed mass, host deficit, duty cycle and environment that independent seed models do not produce. Similar redness alone is not enough.

A closed exchange ledger

The model must choose whether energy crosses. If it does, \(J_{-+}^{\nu}\) must balance on both branches without negative-mass shortcuts or double counting accretion. If it does not, all luminosity must be funded locally.

An orientation trace

If the passage from \(-1\) to positive measure reverses orientation, the map should leave a fixed parity, handedness or phase relation in a statistical sample. The effect must be stated before looking at the data and separated from ordinary rotational selection.

A way to lose

If ordinary seed formation explains every residual; if no stable crossing map exists; if the predicted handedness, redshift window or population covariance is absent; or if the energy budget fails, the inherited-residue explanation should be abandoned.

The TTI reading, without costume

The Dot May Be a Memory Learning to Shine Again

The metaphysical image is now stronger. A universe closes but does not become nothing. Its final residue loses positive extension, traverses the interval before zero, and returns not as the same collection of things but as a bias toward becoming. At a few early knots, that memory gathers a new scaffold and the boundary itself shines. The little red dot is both a visible verb—integrating—and a possible echo: remembering without remaining identical.

◆ Those verbs belong to the philosophy of the framework. The proposal does not establish personal survival, communication from another world, or observational access to a spirit realm. Astronomy measures spectra, structure and dynamics. It could test only the physical shell around the image: a crossing law, conservation rule, orientation trace, population covariance and inheritance clock.

Current astronomy in one paragraph

The Conventional Account Remains the Benchmark

The best-observed little red dots are increasingly consistent with accreting black holes enclosed by dense gas that absorbs, scatters and reprocesses their light; some have directly measured central masses, and faint stellar hosts are beginning to emerge in deeper or stacked imaging. The population is probably not perfectly uniform. None of this requires a \(\chi\)-transition, a previous universe or a \((-1,0)\) sector. ◆

That is enough current theory for this page. Its purpose is not to reproduce the full astronomical debate but to expose what TTI would add, which parts merely rename known physics, and where the addition could be tested.

  • NASA Webb: the early little-red-dot population and black-hole evidence
  • Rusakov et al.: young black holes in dense ionised cocoons
  • Juodžbalis et al.: a direct black-hole mass in one little red dot
  • Zhang et al.: extended stellar hosts around stacked little red dots

TTI’s proposed answer is therefore not “the black holes are illusions” and not “standard cosmology is broken”. It is that a young black hole and its dense red cocoon may be the positive-side machinery through which an older residue becomes locally measurable: the interval from \(-1\) to zero supplies the inherited condition, while the \(\chi\)-front supplies its luminous body. The previous-universe account therefore extends the integration-front account; it does not replace the observed gas, accretion or host galaxy.
