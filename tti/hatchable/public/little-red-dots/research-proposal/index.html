A staged test of a conjecture · derive first, fit second, predict third

The Zero-Seam Test

A research proposal for asking whether JWST’s little red dots are fully explained by young black holes, dense gas and rapidly assembling hosts—or whether their population retains a measurable signature of an integration front carrying inherited cosmic residue.

Proposal abstract

Test the Additional Claim, Not the Metaphor

Little red dots are compact sources in the early universe whose spectra increasingly support accreting black holes embedded in dense gas. TTI accepts that astrophysical account. Its additional conjecture is that some objects are positive-side integration fronts, and that those fronts may be seeded by a global residue represented on an orientation-reversed interval \(-1<\xi<0\).

This programme would not search a telescope image for “another universe”. It would first turn the conjecture into a low-parameter generative model, then test whether that model predicts a preregistered combination of abundance, central-to-host growth, environment, energy balance and—if resolvable—orientation better than conventional astrophysical models. The decisive evidence would be successful prediction in data withheld by field and by observing programme, not an improved fit to the sample used to invent the model.

The null result is valuable: if the crossing law cannot be derived, conserved and made predictive before the held-out data are opened, the inherited-residue account is not yet a scientific explanation.

◆ observations and standard methods · ◆ proposed TTI mechanism. No current observation establishes a previous universe, a physical negative interval or transfer across zero.

The hypotheses

Three Models Must Enter, and Any May Lose

\(H_{\rm A}\): astrophysical baseline. A little red dot is generated by ordinary structure formation, black-hole seeding and growth, accretion, dense-gas radiative transfer and stellar host assembly. Flexible nuisance parameters are allowed, but their priors and complexity penalty must be fixed before the test set is opened. ◆

\(H_{\chi}\): local integration front. The same physical components are present, with one additional local \(\chi\)-evolution law that predicts when a compact source leads the growth of its luminous host. There is no inherited negative-side source. ◆

\(H_{-+}\): inherited-residue front. The local \(\chi\)-front is driven or biased by one global residue projected from \(-1<\xi<0\) through a fixed crossing kernel. This model must improve prediction by more than the penalty for its additional structure. ◆

\[ H_{\rm A}\subset H_{\chi}\subset H_{-+}, \qquad \text{comparison target: }p(D_{\rm held\ out}\mid H), \text{ not }p(D_{\rm training}\mid H). \]

The nesting prevents a common escape. If an inherited-residue prediction fails while the local integration model survives, the result supports neither a previous universe nor the \((-1,0)\) sector. If both additions fail, the conventional model remains the benchmark.

Four research aims

From Mathematical Admissibility to Blind Prediction

Aim 1 — Derive the crossing law

Specify the negative-side state space, its measure, the seam identification and a finite-dimensional kernel \(\mathcal K_{-+}\). Derive the allowed coupling to \(\chi\) from one action or junction rule. No source-specific free parameter may be introduced.

Aim 2 — Build a reproducible census

Construct a selection-aware catalogue from public JWST imaging and spectroscopy, with matched compact AGN and galaxy controls at comparable redshift, luminosity and data quality. Publish inclusion, exclusion and uncertainty rules.

Aim 3 — Test population predictions

Fit all three hypotheses only to training fields. Compare their predictions for abundance, black-hole and host growth, compactness, spectral state, environment and energy accounting in untouched fields.

Aim 4 — Make a prospective forecast

Use the archival result to select a small matched follow-up sample and publish object-level forecasts before the decisive observations are reduced. This is the main protection against retrospective pattern-finding.

The minimum mathematical object

A Crossing Kernel with No Object-by-Object Escape Hatch

Let \(r_-(\xi,y)\) be the global residue state and let \(J_+(x,t)\) be its proposed positive-side source. The research model begins with

\[ J_+(x,t;\phi)=g\!\int_{-1}^{0}\!d\xi\int d\mu_-(y)\, \mathcal K_{-+}(x,t;\xi,y\mid\phi)\,r_-(\xi,y), \]

where \(\phi\) is one population-level parameter vector shared by every source. The local integration field obeys

\[ \frac{D\chi}{Dt}=F_{\rm TTI}[\chi,g_{\mu\nu},\rho,T]+J_+(x,t;\phi), \qquad \Sigma_{\rm LRD}=\left\{x:\frac12<\chi<1, \frac{D\chi}{Dt}>0,|\nabla\chi|>\chi_{\rm c}\right\}. \]

The theory group must map \(\phi\) to observables before fitting. A schematic generative layer is

\[ \mathbf y_i =\mathcal A(\mathbf x_i;\theta) +\mathcal B[J_+(x_i,t_i;\phi)] +\boldsymbol\epsilon_i, \]

where \(\mathcal A\) is the conventional astrophysical model, \(\mathbf x_i\) contains measured conditions and selection variables, and \(\mathbf y_i\) contains the preregistered outcomes. The operator \(\mathcal B\) cannot be replaced by a separate latent adjustment for every little red dot; doing so would make \(H_{-+}\) unfalsifiable.

Observable consequences

Five Signatures, One Declared Primary Test

No single red spectrum is diagnostic. The proposal lives or dies through a constrained pattern across the population.

1. The inheritance clock

The crossing solution must predict a redshift-dependent source term and therefore an onset, peak and decline in little-red-dot abundance after the documented selection function is applied. An arbitrary curve fitted to the observed decline does not count.

2. Central-to-host lag

The same clock must predict how black-hole mass, compact-source luminosity and extended host light approach one another with cosmic time. This tests the claim that a closure-ready centre precedes its luminous host.

3. Shared-residue covariance

One global residue sampled many times should impose a low-rank relation among source mass, host deficit, size, duty state and environment after redshift and selection are controlled. The covariance and its sign must be fixed on training data.

4. A closed energy audit

An information-only model predicts no unexplained energy excess. A mass-energy-transfer model must predict one and balance it through \(J_{-+}^{\nu}\). Accretion luminosity may not be counted again as seam luminosity.

5. Orientation, only if resolvable

An orientation-reversing kernel may predict a parity or handedness statistic in resolved hosts or outflows. Because most dots are barely resolved and ordinary selection can mimic asymmetry, this is a secondary test unless image simulations demonstrate adequate recovery.

Primary test

The preregistered primary endpoint is the joint held-out predictive score for the inheritance clock, central-to-host lag and shared covariance. Energy and orientation remain mandatory consistency tests, but cannot rescue a failed primary result.

Data and measurement plan

Begin with Public Observations, Then Earn New Telescope Time

Imaging. Use public JWST multi-band imaging to measure compact-source flux, colour, effective radius, extended host fraction and local environment with injection-and-recovery tests. Stacked measurements may inform population parameters but may not be assigned to individual objects.

Spectroscopy. Use public spectra with reliable redshifts to measure line ratios, widths, asymmetries, continuum shape and dense-gas indicators. Broadening by electron scattering, kinematics and instrumental response must be compared before any residual is assigned to TTI.

External constraints. Where available, use X-ray, submillimetre, radio or lensing data to constrain obscuration, gas, star formation and mass. Missing coverage is modelled as missingness, not as a non-detection.

Controls. Match little red dots to compact AGN and galaxies by redshift, depth, magnification, luminosity and usable wavelength coverage. The control pipeline must be identical to the target pipeline.

Sample size. Fix the final archival and prospective sample sizes through mock-catalogue recovery simulations before opening held-out fields. The power calculation must include field variance, contamination, incompleteness and correlated measurement error; a convenient round number is not a design.

Analysis discipline

How the Study Avoids Finding What It Put In

Split by field

Entire survey fields or observing programmes, not random objects, are withheld. This limits leakage from shared depths, calibrations and large-scale structure.

Forward-model selection

Each hypothesis generates intrinsic populations that pass through the same detection, colour, morphology and spectroscopic-selection model before comparison with the catalogue.

Penalise flexibility

Report held-out log predictive density, calibration and posterior-predictive checks. More parameters do not win merely because they improve the training fit.

Blind the decisive labels

For the prospective phase, freeze candidate selection, model code and forecasts before the decisive host or spectral measurements are revealed to the modelling team.

Run null injections

Analyse simulated universes containing only conventional black holes and gas. The pipeline must not manufacture a residue signature from selection effects or model mismatch.

Publish every branch

Report the information-only, energy-transfer and junction-only versions separately. The study may not switch between them after seeing which observable is inconvenient.

Conservation gate

Choose What Crosses Before Counting What Shines

The proposal requires one of three mutually distinguishable mechanisms:

  1. Information or topology only: the residue changes boundary conditions, while all measurable mass-energy is assembled locally.
  2. Mass-energy exchange: the crossing current obeys \(\nabla_\mu T_+^{\mu\nu}=J_{-+}^{\nu}\) and \(\nabla_\mu T_-^{\mu\nu}=-J_{-+}^{\nu}\), with a defined global accounting surface.
  3. Junction only: nothing propagates between sectors; both satisfy one global boundary condition that correlates their solutions.

Failure to choose is a stop condition. In particular, a negative coordinate does not imply negative mass, and an unexplained luminosity cannot be called accretion in one calculation and inherited energy in another.

Decision rule

What Would Count as Progress?

Continue developing \(H_{-+}\) only if one conserved crossing model, fixed before unblinding, improves joint prediction in independent fields and its prospective object-level forecasts are calibrated. Even then, the result would identify an unexplained population law consistent with the model—not prove a previous universe.

Retain only \(H_{\chi}\) if a local integration law predicts the source–host sequence but no inherited-residue covariance or clock survives.

Prefer \(H_{\rm A}\) if conventional astrophysics matches or outpredicts the added models, if the crossing kernel cannot be derived, or if conservation requires hidden object-specific adjustments.

Reject the tested implementation if its prespecified redshift, covariance or energy predictions fail. A failed implementation need not disprove every imaginable cyclic cosmology; it does remove this version from scientific use.

People, tools, and outputs

A Small Interdisciplinary Collaboration

Theory

Mathematical relativity, field theory and topology expertise to derive the seam map, dimensions and conservation law rather than infer them from the desired answer.

Observation

Early-universe, AGN, stellar-population and radiative-transfer expertise to construct the baseline and prevent unfamiliar gas physics from being relabelled as new cosmology.

Inference

Selection-function, simulation and predictive-model-comparison expertise, with an independent analyst responsible for the held-out evaluation.

Phase I requires archival access, reproducible computing and staff time rather than immediate new observing time. A Phase II telescope proposal should be submitted only after the archival gate fixes the targets, measurements and required sensitivity.

Observational foundation

Starting Literature

The proposal begins from the evidence summarised in the parent conjecture: an early compact population, widespread signs of accreting black holes, dense ionised cocoons, at least one direct dynamical black-hole mass, and faint extended hosts in stacked imaging. These observations motivate the study but do not support its TTI extension by themselves.

  • NASA Webb: population census and accretion evidence
  • Rusakov et al.: young black holes in dense ionised cocoons
  • Juodžbalis et al.: direct Keplerian black-hole mass
  • Zhang et al.: extended host components in stacked imaging

The proposal’s standard is deliberately severe: TTI must predict a constrained relation that ordinary black-hole and galaxy physics did not already supply, and it must do so before the decisive observations are examined.
