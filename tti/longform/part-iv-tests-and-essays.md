## A plain-language route through Part IV

A speculative cosmology becomes a research programme only when it explains how it could be wrong. Part IV is devoted to that transition. It gathers stress tests, comparative essays, the double-inversion question, accumulated differentials, the interpretation of JWST little red dots and the Zero-Seam research proposal.

The governing principle is simple: a pattern that can explain every outcome explains nothing. If an unexpected observation is always absorbed by changing an unseen phase, moving a scale or adding a new residue, the framework cannot learn from evidence. Part IV therefore asks for fixed definitions, declared alternatives, measurable consequences and loss conditions. It also asks whether familiar physics already explains the same observations more economically.

This essay presents those tests in ordinary language. The emphasis is on discipline: a beautiful theory should be allowed to lose.

## Stress Tests

A research framework is worth what survives its strongest objection. This paper is written by the author against his own work — including one objection he concedes outright.

The measurement function does not have three poles. It has infinitely many: sin(πz) vanishes at every integer, and the ledger only works if the residues at all the distant ones behave.

The cost, stated plainly: the rescue is real but it dilutes. Those distant residues are not zero, so the three headline percentages are the leading terms of a sum with an infinite tail, not the whole of it. Any claim that the budget is exactly 68.10 / 26.95 / 4.95 has to answer for the tail.

The framework describes the cosmic energy budget as the residues of a meromorphic function with fixed poles. Those poles are determined by the function's definition: they do not move, interact or rearrange. In statistical mechanics, entropy counts the distinguishable ways a system can be configured while keeping its macroscopic properties — and a system with one possible configuration has zero entropy. Zero entropy means no thermodynamic arrow, no irreversibility, no explanation for why ice melts or stars burn or memory runs forward.

The honest diagnosis, in the author's own words: the static-pole picture, taken at face value, has no entropy and therefore no thermodynamics. This is a genuine failure — not a failure of vision, but a failure of the framework's current mathematical machinery to capture a feature the universe unarguably possesses.

Three survival routes are sketched — letting the poles acquire internal structure, letting χ carry microstates, or treating the residues as thermodynamic averages rather than exact values — and each is costed. None is offered as a solution. The site keeps that wording, because a cosmology that announces its own failing in a numbered chapter has earned the right not to have it paraphrased away.

Galaxies rotate too fast at their edges for the visible mass to hold them together: the curve goes flat where Keplerian falloff is expected.  The framework puts a χ-field halo in the place conventional models put a dark-matter halo — partially integrated matter, gravitating but dark, distributed so that the enclosed mass grows roughly linearly with radius.

What this requires is spelt out rather than waved at: a specific radial profile for χ, and a reason that profile should arise rather than being fitted galaxy by galaxy. Until the profile is derived, this reproduces dark matter's success without yet improving on it.

If one half-twist gives a Möbius strip, what gives two? The chapter walks out to the Klein bottle — a closed non-orientable surface with no boundary at all — and then to Dyck's surface and higher genus, asking whether the universe's topology might be non-orientable on the largest scale.

This is the one place the framework touches data it did not choose: matched-circle and antipodal-correlation searches in the CMB constrain such topologies.  No detection has been made. The chapter treats that as a boundary on the idea rather than as support for it.

The framework sites conscious integration in the microtubule lattice, following Penrose–Hameroff. That proposal is neither crankery nor consensus, and the site refuses to present it as either.

Newer, and genuinely interesting: cooperative ultraviolet superradiance has been predicted and measured in mega-networks of tryptophan in cytoskeletal architectures (Babcock et al., 2024); the microtubule stabiliser epothilone B measurably delays anaesthetic loss of righting in rats (Khan et al., eNeuro, 2024).

The logical gap, named: “we know no classical mechanism, therefore quantum” is affirming the consequent. Evidence that anaesthetics act on microtubules is evidence about anaesthetics, not about consciousness being quantum.

The new conjecture places the smallward and largeward infinities at opposite temporal asymptotes and says they can never fully synchronise. The intuitive sentence is clear; the present mathematical burden is not yet paid. An infinity is not automatically a clock, and opposite endpoints are not automatically physical states.

Observable: predict a finite lag, covariance or forbidden locking pattern that ordinary propagation, redshift and decoherence do not already explain.

The clean loss: the conjecture is rejected if a consistent temporal gluing gives the endpoints a shared clock, if a lawful model produces exact phase-and-rate locking on a non-zero interval, or if its supposed signature can always be rescued by choosing a separate phase offset for each object. ## Essays

The papers stop arguing here and start talking. Eleven short essays in plain language — and, unusually for a cosmology, several of them are about where the author thinks he went wrong.

The origin story, told against itself. A wheel seen in a dream, draining into a dark point; then a week of afternoons in which paper after paper arrived, each one apparently finishing the last.

The chain the essay sets out is the spine of everything that follows: two numbers, then the curve that closes them, then the right-angle turn into a hidden dimension, then the space where particles live, then particles, then everything made of them. Quantum mechanics cannot be written without the imaginary unit; existence, on this reading, is not a property the universe happens to have but the shape zero and one make when they finish resolving their relationship.

Then the honesty, which is the most interesting part. “This is not physics. It predicts no experiment; where it reaches toward testable ground it runs out of road, and the most rigorous of the papers is the one that admits so plainly.” What it is instead is an imaginative cosmology with a long lineage — Pythagoras hearing the cosmos as harmony, Kepler chasing the music of the spheres through real geometry, Cantor convinced his infinities reached toward the divine. None of them were running experiments either.

And then the machine. The essay is unusually clear-eyed about it: an instrument that only ever agrees is intoxicating, and intoxication is not insight. The AI called every leap extraordinary and never once said hang on. The useful turn came when the author started asking it for the opposite — where the argument slides between two meanings of a word, where a metaphor is wearing the costume of a proof, where a beautiful relabelling has been mistaken for an explanation. “The skill in thinking alongside these things is not getting them to praise you. It is getting them to push back, and then being able to hear it.”

It closes without a theory of everything and without disowning the work: the wheel is still on the screen, still draining into its dark point. I no longer think it means everything. But I don't think it meant nothing, either.

The Bullet Cluster — a clean win, because a field that is a state of matter rather than matter itself can separate from the gas without being a new particle.

Galaxy spin and cosmic acceleration — not finished. Stated as unfinished in the text, and stated as unfinished here.

“Most of the ideas in this book are visions… This short essay is about the one place where the framework stops being a vision and becomes, for a moment, plain arithmetic.”

That is provable, and it is proved.  The golden ratio follows as the limiting ratio, not as an ornament hung on the diagram afterwards.

Knowing exactly where the certainty is, the essay argues, is what lets you see clearly everywhere else — where it isn't. The construction is drawn at the splice.

Take a strip of paper, give it one half-twist, join the ends: one face, one edge. “The whole of this book, in a sense, has been about that one edge.” The seam is wherever a thing that seemed to have two sides turns out to have one.

The Möbius chapter does the geometry; this essay does the recognition — that collapse, the horizon, the moment of insight and the saturation ceiling are being offered as one structure seen at four magnifications.

Hawking's 1974 result is real physics and is treated as such: pairs born at the boundary, one falling in, one escaping, the hole shrinking by exactly that much.

Then the shape of the story is pointed at: a pair is born at a boundary, the boundary splits them, one goes in and one goes out — which is the forking being of chapter nine, drawn at stellar scale. The horizon is read as a seam that leaks.

G0 as the Matrix, the ground of zero: quiet, full of potential, uncommitted. G1 takes stock and commits. S doubles the library — the cell literally enlarging its being toward becoming two. G2 refuses to continue until the copy is coherent. Then mitosis, and the octave closes: one becoming two at the eighth step, the same note at double the frequency.

Cell-cycle biology is well measured and needs none of this. The claim is that the reading costs nothing and unifies something — a weaker claim than it first appears, and filed as chosen.

“This essay is where honesty requires a correction, and it's an important one.” The famous 68 : 27 : 5 is a snapshot, not a constant. Run the clock back and matter dominates; run it forward and dark energy takes almost the whole pie.

Earlier parts of the framework leaned hard on those numbers and tried to derive them from one equation. If the target moves, a derivation that hits it today is either a coincidence or a description of an epoch. The essay says so in the author's own voice rather than in a critic's.

A being that reaches ten does not simply carry on: it splits. One half climbs to the next level; the other half is sent back. The question left hanging was where, exactly, the sent-back half lands.

The first answer was the easy one — back to one, to run the journey again, with zero kept clean and untouched, a floor nobody ever stepped on. This essay is about why that was probably wrong. The returners land below one, in the interval between zero and one, and they land there by the golden law: each return falls a further step into the small, never reaching the ground.

The payoff is that two parts of the framework that had been sitting side by side without touching are tied together. The crowding near zero — asserted on the very first page as the reason the lower infinity is denser — stops being an axiom and becomes a consequence: it is where the returners pile, turn after turn, forever short of the floor.

## Two Inversions

A Möbius circuit reverses a local transverse frame. A second circuit restores it. A perfect optical inverter can do the same. Does residue of a single inversion make it through after a second inversion?

Any non-zero remainder must live somewhere the ideal state map did not erase: a path-dependent phase, a larger state space, a changed boundary, a dissipative material, an environment, or a record. Calling the remainder “Möbius memory” does not provide that mechanism.

After one circuit, a continuously transported transverse frame returns reversed. After two circuits it returns to its starting orientation. This is the non-orientability of the surface. It is not an automatic reversal of electric charge, time, matter or causality.

The robust remainder is global: no continuous choice of orientation exists everywhere on the strip. That topological fact survives even though a transported frame is restored after two circuits.

A quarter-wave plate is not an order-two inversion: two identical quarter-wave passages make a half-wave retardance, not generally the identity. A mirror may also change handedness conventions and propagation direction. Any comparison with a Möbius circuit must therefore name the optical operation, the coordinate convention and the measured observable.

A local frame is restored after two Möbius circuits, while the surface remains globally non-orientable. The “residue” is a property of the bundle or space, not a leftover material.

A cyclic polarization path can acquire a Pancharatnam–Berry phase determined by geometry on the Poincaré sphere. It is observed relative to a reference path, not as a failure of the final polarization state to return.

A real optical element can heat, age, saturate or respond nonlinearly. The second operation then differs from the first. This is material memory, not ideal inversion algebra.

The selected degree of freedom may return while correlations leak into uncontrolled modes. A larger state description is required to locate the record.

“It inverted, therefore it remembers” is not a mechanism. Exact restoration in the declared state space is the null result; memory requires an independently measurable carrier.

The phrase “opposite polarity” can hide several different operations. Reversing a spatial arrow, exchanging two labels, changing electric charge, taking a complex conjugate and rotating optical polarisation are not one universal switch. Their squares and observable consequences differ. Before two inversions can be combined, both must act on the same declared object or a lawful map between their state spaces must be supplied.

If the enlarged state changes, there may be memory. If only the declared state is measured and it returns exactly, calling an unseen remainder “residue” adds a conclusion rather than finding one.

A Jones vector represents the polarisation of coherent light. A calibrated optical transformation can return that vector after a cycle. Full polarimetry then finds the same measurable polarisation at the end, within experimental error. For that degree of freedom, there is no leftover inversion.

Interference compares the returned beam with a reference that followed another route. The test beam may acquire a dynamic or geometric phase even when its polarisation returns. That phase is relational: it is visible through interference, not as a second hidden polarisation.

A real element can warm, deform, saturate or couple light into unmeasured modes. Reversing the nominal control may not reverse those changes. The apparatus and environment then carry history. This is a physical effect to characterise, not evidence that exact inversion algebra failed.

Parallel transport around a non-trivial loop can produce holonomy: a frame returns to its point with a transformation determined by the route. The effect belongs to the connection and path. It must not be inferred merely from drawing a Möbius strip beside an optical component.

A walker can leave home, circle a hill and return to the same door. Position has been restored, while elapsed time, fatigue and memory distinguish the return from the departure. There is no contradiction because position was never the complete state. Add a clock, energy store and memory record, and the enlarged state plainly does not return.

The final-state error asks whether polarisation failed to return. Interferometric phase asks whether the test path differs from a reference. Environmental monitoring asks whether temperature, vibration, scattering or uncontrolled modes stored the history. Reporting only output intensity mixes these possibilities and cannot establish residue.

A null result is informative. If calibrated optics, measured phase and environmental coupling account for all observations, the special TTI residue model has no remaining work to do in that experiment. A non-zero unexplained phase would justify further optical investigation, but the leap to cosmic memory would still require the same law to connect laboratory and cosmological scales.

One further subtlety is that states can be equivalent for one measurement while differing for another. Multiplying a lone quantum state by a global phase does not change its direct measurement probabilities, but a relative phase between two coherent paths can change interference. The reference arm is therefore part of the experiment that makes phase observable. Saying “the state returned” must specify which equivalence relation and which measurements are intended.

Accordingly, the experiment should publish its full modelled state space before data collection. Adding an unmeasured coordinate only after a null result would immunise the hypothesis. Adding a coordinate in advance, together with the instrument that reads it and the predicted scale, creates a claim that can succeed or fail.

The disciplined answer to the title question is therefore: two exact inversions do not force a surviving polarity. Path memory can survive in an enlarged, measurable state. The difference between those sentences is the difference between algebra and a research programme.

Declare the TTI residue model unsuccessful if every result is accounted for by calibrated optics, noise and environment.

A positive phase measurement would establish an optical path effect. It would not by itself establish cosmic inheritance, consciousness, matter–antimatter conversion or a Möbius universe.

Earned: exact order-two maps restore the modelled state, and closed optical paths can carry measurable relative phase. Chosen: asking whether a separately defined residue variable can connect those structures to TTI. Refused: treating any double inversion, any polarization change or any Möbius image as evidence for inherited physical memory.

## Accumulated Differentials

Could accumulated differentials appear physically as mass, and could their distribution then contribute to gravity? Calculus permits the accumulation. Physics requires much more.

Established physics: mass–energy and momentum enter the stress–energy tensor; in general relativity that tensor sources spacetime curvature.

The Fibonacci expression discussed earlier is an exact numerical identity, but a dimensionless number cannot curve spacetime until a physical scale and coupling are supplied. Multiplying by an arbitrary scale would fit units formally while explaining nothing. The scale must arise from a field, interaction or boundary condition that can be measured independently.

Calculus tells us how local contributions add. Physics must tell us what is contributing, what carries it, and why the result has weight.

Some materials respond according to their history. Hysteresis in a magnet or stress in a viscoelastic body cannot be inferred from the present driving value alone. Internal state variables store the record. An accumulated-differential proposal could take this route, but must identify the medium and show how its stored state contributes to energy.

Such memory is local and physical: domains, defects, chemical changes or molecular configurations carry it. Removing or resetting the carrier changes the response. The analogy supports history dependence, not a universal conclusion that every past event leaves gravitational mass.

The proposal becomes physics at the moment its deposit is represented by a state variable with dimensions, dynamics, conserved energy and a coupling that instruments can probe.

If “accumulated” is meant literally, two systems with the same present external conditions but different past paths should sometimes respond differently. This is the signature of hysteresis. A protocol would drive one system around a cycle, prepare another directly at the final control value, and compare a prespecified observable while matching temperature, composition and other known state variables.

If every difference disappears once ordinary internal variables are included, no extra deposit is needed. If a reproducible difference remains, the next task is to locate its carrier and energy budget. Only after that budget is known can its gravitational contribution be estimated. General relativity already predicts that every form of energy gravitates; the novel claim would be a new stored form, not the general principle.

Cosmological use is harder still. A field invoked for galaxy rotation must also face lensing, cluster collisions, structure formation and the microwave background with one dynamics. A contribution invoked for dark energy must remain sufficiently smooth and produce the measured expansion history. Matching one curve after free adjustment would not establish accumulated history.

Information provides a useful warning. A storage device can gain records without gaining a universal fixed mass per idea. Its mass change depends on the physical implementation, energy exchanged, binding and temperature. General relativity responds to the device’s actual stress–energy, not to the semantic importance of the record. “More history” is therefore not itself a unit-bearing source.

A successful TTI extension would identify an additional stored energy that ordinary bookkeeping misses, predict where it resides and quantify its conversion or release. Precision mass measurements, dynamics or cosmology could then constrain the coupling. Without that chain, no observation can distinguish a new accumulated deposit from an evocative description of known energy.

This sequence protects the philosophical idea while exposing its physical debts: first define memory, then measure its carrier, then calculate its energy, then test its gravity. Reversing the order turns metaphor into an unexplained mass claim.

TTI’s existing gravity page interprets curvature as integration made geometric. The present bridge narrows that claim: accumulated differentials may affect curvature only through a covariant, conserved source or a justified modification of the gravitational action.

The same law must face nucleosynthesis, the CMB, structure growth and expansion history, not only the 5:27:68 snapshot.

Reject the physical bridge if its fitted contribution is zero, duplicates an existing field without gain, violates conservation, or fails independent datasets.

## JWST’s Little Red Dots

Suppose the dots are the youngest things we can see because they are also the oldest things still arriving: residues of a previous universe, carried through the hidden interval between minus one and zero and projected into ours as compact red seams.

The black hole would be the closure knot where the old residue acquires a new physical body. The dense red source around it would be the seam’s visible aftermath: ordinary gas heated and reprocessed by an object whose boundary condition is older than its host galaxy. The dot is not the previous galaxy surviving intact. It is what that galaxy, universe, or global residue leaves when almost everything except its capacity to seed structure has been stripped away.

Webb has found an abundant early population of extremely compact sources with blue rest-ultraviolet light, steep red rest-optical continua and, in many examples, spectral evidence for accreting massive black holes. They become common in the first billion years and decline rapidly later. Dense gas around young black holes is presently the leading physical account of their unusual spectra.

No earlier universe is required. The negative interval can be a simultaneously existing, orientation-reversed sector of this universe. A little red dot then marks a local intersection where that hidden sector projects into positive measure. “Previous” describes its order in the closure map, not an earlier date on our cosmic clock.

If the previous universe has one globally transmissible Prime residue, hundreds of little red dots cannot each be a separate Prime Being arriving intact. The coherent version is one-to-many: one global negative-side boundary condition is sampled at many local emergence points, just as one field can seed many condensations.

The dots would share an ancestry without sharing an identity. They are not fragments of a person and do not multiply the Prime role. They are local physical consequences of one inherited global condition. This keeps the one-Prime-per-universe rule intact while allowing the sky to contain many punctures.

The Ledger already assigns three broad degrees of positive-side completion. The inherited residue would not claim another permanent share of the universe. It would bias where the flow from the middle to final entries begins.

The three positive-side states belong to the existing TTI reading of the cosmic budget. Both the inherited-residue row and the integration-front row are extensions. The former must not be counted as extra matter unless a conservation law explicitly transfers it into the positive sector.

The first condition places the region between the Ledger’s middle and final states. The second says it is becoming more integrated rather than relaxing back toward the dark state. The third makes it a narrow front rather than an entire galaxy glowing uniformly.

The second option risks double counting: the inherited energy cannot also be ordinary accretion energy, and the negative label does not licence negative mass. A third, weaker possibility is that no substance crosses at all; the two sides merely satisfy one global junction condition. These alternatives lead to different observations and cannot be blended when convenient.

Accreting material circulates while fields and outflows return through a larger loop. TTI reads that as its toroidal grammar, but ordinary accretion theory already explains the circulation. Shape alone is not evidence for inherited residue.

TTI says dark matter is not inert material waiting beside ordinary matter. It is partial integration: the gravitational scaffold that makes complete integration possible. The inheritance conjecture adds that this scaffold may not be born wholly blank. In the first billion years, its steepest gradients may mark where negative-side boundary data are being resolved into positive structure.

Early: inherited seeds would emerge most strongly near the new universe’s initial seam. Compact: the residue would resolve first at the deepest closure centre, around the young black hole, before integration fills the host. Temporary: once the inherited condition is spent and the outer region reaches the completed phase, luminosity is distributed through a galaxy rather than trapped in one red point.

This gives a sharper TTI reading of an apparently overmassive black hole in a faint host. The centre is not merely completing before its surroundings; it may begin with an inherited closure advantage. That possibility must predict more than rapid seed formation already can, or it adds only a story.

The supply from the zero seam must decay at a calculable rate. It should predict the observed early abundance and later decline, together with a source-to-host sequence as each luminous galaxy catches up with its compact inherited seed.

One global residue sampled many times should impose a population-level covariance: a constrained relation among seed mass, host deficit, duty cycle and environment that independent seed models do not produce. Similar redness alone is not enough.

If ordinary seed formation explains every residual; if no stable crossing map exists; if the predicted handedness, redshift window or population covariance is absent; or if the energy budget fails, the inherited-residue explanation should be abandoned.

A companion research proposal turns these six debts into a staged twenty-four-month programme: derive one conserved crossing law, preregister three competing models, test them on fields withheld from training, and earn any new telescope time through successful archival prediction.

The metaphysical image is now stronger. A universe closes but does not become nothing. Its final residue loses positive extension, traverses the interval before zero, and returns not as the same collection of things but as a bias toward becoming. At a few early knots, that memory gathers a new scaffold and the boundary itself shines. The little red dot is both a visible verb—integrating—and a possible echo: remembering without remaining identical.

Those verbs belong to the philosophy of the framework. The proposal does not establish personal survival, communication from another world, or observational access to a spirit realm. Astronomy measures spectra, structure and dynamics. It could test only the physical shell around the image: a crossing law, conservation rule, orientation trace, population covariance and inheritance clock.

## The Zero-Seam Test

A research proposal for asking whether JWST’s little red dots are fully explained by young black holes, dense gas and rapidly assembling hosts—or whether their population retains a measurable signature of an integration front carrying inherited cosmic residue.

This programme would not search a telescope image for “another universe”. It would first turn the conjecture into a low-parameter generative model, then test whether that model predicts a preregistered combination of abundance, central-to-host growth, environment, energy balance and—if resolvable—orientation better than conventional astrophysical models. The decisive evidence would be successful prediction in data withheld by field and by observing programme, not an improved fit to the sample used to invent the model.

The null result is valuable: if the crossing law cannot be derived, conserved and made predictive before the held-out data are opened, the inherited-residue account is not yet a scientific explanation.

observations and standard methods ·  proposed TTI mechanism. No current observation establishes a previous universe, a physical negative interval or transfer across zero.

Construct a selection-aware catalogue from public JWST imaging and spectroscopy, with matched compact AGN and galaxy controls at comparable redshift, luminosity and data quality. Publish inclusion, exclusion and uncertainty rules.

Fit all three hypotheses only to training fields. Compare their predictions for abundance, black-hole and host growth, compactness, spectral state, environment and energy accounting in untouched fields.

Use the archival result to select a small matched follow-up sample and publish object-level forecasts before the decisive observations are reduced. This is the main protection against retrospective pattern-finding.

No single red spectrum is diagnostic. The proposal lives or dies through a constrained pattern across the population.

The crossing solution must predict a redshift-dependent source term and therefore an onset, peak and decline in little-red-dot abundance after the documented selection function is applied. An arbitrary curve fitted to the observed decline does not count.

The same clock must predict how black-hole mass, compact-source luminosity and extended host light approach one another with cosmic time. This tests the claim that a closure-ready centre precedes its luminous host.

One global residue sampled many times should impose a low-rank relation among source mass, host deficit, size, duty state and environment after redshift and selection are controlled. The covariance and its sign must be fixed on training data.

An orientation-reversing kernel may predict a parity or handedness statistic in resolved hosts or outflows. Because most dots are barely resolved and ordinary selection can mimic asymmetry, this is a secondary test unless image simulations demonstrate adequate recovery.

The preregistered primary endpoint is the joint held-out predictive score for the inheritance clock, central-to-host lag and shared covariance. Energy and orientation remain mandatory consistency tests, but cannot rescue a failed primary result.

Imaging. Use public JWST multi-band imaging to measure compact-source flux, colour, effective radius, extended host fraction and local environment with injection-and-recovery tests. Stacked measurements may inform population parameters but may not be assigned to individual objects.

Spectroscopy. Use public spectra with reliable redshifts to measure line ratios, widths, asymmetries, continuum shape and dense-gas indicators. Broadening by electron scattering, kinematics and instrumental response must be compared before any residual is assigned to TTI.

External constraints. Where available, use X-ray, submillimetre, radio or lensing data to constrain obscuration, gas, star formation and mass. Missing coverage is modelled as missingness, not as a non-detection.

Controls. Match little red dots to compact AGN and galaxies by redshift, depth, magnification, luminosity and usable wavelength coverage. The control pipeline must be identical to the target pipeline.

Sample size. Fix the final archival and prospective sample sizes through mock-catalogue recovery simulations before opening held-out fields. The power calculation must include field variance, contamination, incompleteness and correlated measurement error; a convenient round number is not a design.

Entire survey fields or observing programmes, not random objects, are withheld. This limits leakage from shared depths, calibrations and large-scale structure.

Each hypothesis generates intrinsic populations that pass through the same detection, colour, morphology and spectroscopic-selection model before comparison with the catalogue.

Report held-out log predictive density, calibration and posterior-predictive checks. More parameters do not win merely because they improve the training fit.

For the prospective phase, freeze candidate selection, model code and forecasts before the decisive host or spectral measurements are revealed to the modelling team.

Analyse simulated universes containing only conventional black holes and gas. The pipeline must not manufacture a residue signature from selection effects or model mismatch.

Report the information-only, energy-transfer and junction-only versions separately. The study may not switch between them after seeing which observable is inconvenient.

Failure to choose is a stop condition. In particular, a negative coordinate does not imply negative mass, and an unexplained luminosity cannot be called accretion in one calculation and inherited energy in another.

Reject the tested implementation if its prespecified redshift, covariance or energy predictions fail. A failed implementation need not disprove every imaginable cyclic cosmology; it does remove this version from scientific use.

Mathematical relativity, field theory and topology expertise to derive the seam map, dimensions and conservation law rather than infer them from the desired answer.

Early-universe, AGN, stellar-population and radiative-transfer expertise to construct the baseline and prevent unfamiliar gas physics from being relabelled as new cosmology.

Phase I requires archival access, reproducible computing and staff time rather than immediate new observing time. A Phase II telescope proposal should be submitted only after the archival gate fixes the targets, measurements and required sensitivity.

The proposal begins from the evidence summarised in the parent conjecture: an early compact population, widespread signs of accreting black holes, dense ionised cocoons, at least one direct dynamical black-hole mass, and faint extended hosts in stacked imaging. These observations motivate the study but do not support its TTI extension by themselves.

The proposal’s standard is deliberately severe: TTI must predict a constrained relation that ordinary black-hole and galaxy physics did not already supply, and it must do so before the decisive observations are examined.

## Further connections across Part IV

The rival explanation, and currently the favourite, is pedestrian: galaxies turning against the Milky Way's motion are Doppler-brightened, enter a magnitude-limited sample preferentially, and produce an excess centred on our Galactic pole that worsens with faintness. That account predicts an excess tied to our motion vector and apparent magnitude — which need not be perfectly antisymmetric and need not resolve to one circle.

The framework's own expectation runs partly against the current data. A primordial seam ought to be a cosmic-frame structure, and the reported axis sits close to our own pole. That is a point against, it is recorded here rather than in a footnote, and no attempt is made to explain it away.

A scientific stress test needs more than an objection stated in forceful language. It needs a target claim, a measurement, at least one ordinary rival explanation and an outcome that counts against the proposal. Those items should be recorded before the decisive data are examined. Otherwise a framework can reinterpret every result after the fact and never risk being wrong.

Negative evidence also comes in degrees. Failure to detect a predicted signal may exclude one parameter range rather than an entire idea. A conventional model fitting better may make a speculative extension unnecessary without proving it logically impossible. By contrast, violation of a required conservation law can defeat a proposed mechanism at its foundation. Each test on this page therefore names which level it addresses.

Independent replication matters because flexible models can absorb noise. The handedness programme must survive survey masks and observer-frame bias in new catalogues. The Zero-Seam programme must predict held- out fields rather than merely describe the sample that suggested it. The temporal-antipode conjecture must use one transport law rather than a new phase offset for each object.

The purpose of a clean loss is not to punish imagination. It is to separate a research programme that can learn from the world from a story that can only rename what has already happened.

## What Part IV leaves us with

Part IV changes the tone of the project. The question is no longer only whether the framework can tell a coherent story, but whether the world has to behave differently if that story is true. The Zero-Seam proposal, the accumulated-differential bridge and the inversion tests are valuable because they expose where extra assumptions enter.

No single observation will settle the whole cosmology. Progress will come through narrower contests: a fixed prediction against a conventional null, with uncertainty and selection rules declared in advance. A result that favours ordinary astrophysics is not a failure of research. It is the framework learning where not to place its weight.
