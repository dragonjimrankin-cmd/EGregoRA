## Scope

The audit covers all generated TTI documents, shared metadata, local navigation, fragments, structured data, discovery files, the interactive Atlas and its written fallback, the visual plate room, the prepared ShakDrah corpus, and deployment smoke checks.

Run the complete local audit with:

```sh
npm run audit:tti
```

The command performs two Eleventy builds around deterministic plate gathering, validates every generated HTML document, tests the six-Part masthead and matching research labels, the six-path Atlas, the canonical thirty-four-entry project contents, per-document records and outline shells, the thirty-two site-wide editorial guides, the exclusive plate accordion, the custom site audit, the six rendered Part syntheses, the preserved final-synthesis source and the prepared browser-side knowledge corpus. It keeps the twenty-seven original Parts I–IV research essays within 2,000–4,000 readable words, independently enforces 6,000–8,000 words for every Part synthesis and 10,000–13,000 words for the retained final project source.

## Boundaries of this audit

The local audit does not claim that the TTI physics is correct, that a previous universe exists, that the negative interval is a physical domain, that the two scale-infinities are physical clocks at opposite temporal ends, that entropy equals TTI integration, that an exact double inversion retains physical residue, or that accumulated differentials generate mass or gravity. It does not claim a proof of RH, global complete monotonicity, positivity of the proposed Bernstein density, or a physical implication from zeta mathematics to the Skeletal Splice. It checks the site’s internal integrity and whether those claims retain their declared speculative status.

External scientific links are preserved but are not treated as automatically valid forever. The deployment workflow separately verifies selected live pages after Hatchable publishes them. Visual and audible quality still require human inspection even when markup, voice routing and timing metadata pass automated tests.
