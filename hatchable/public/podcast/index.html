For Ed — how to publish a new episode. Drop the MP3 into src/assets/audio/, then add an entry at the end of src/_data/episodes.json: 
{ "number": "004", "title": "…", "date": "2026-10-17", "duration": "1h 04m",
  "summary": "…", "audio": "/assets/audio/ep004.mp3", "tags": ["astronomy"] }
Commit and push — the site rebuilds with the episode and the RSS feed in place. External hosts (Acast, Captivate, Spotify) work too: put their direct MP3 URL in audio.
