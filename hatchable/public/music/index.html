Tuned Air

Music

Drone, modal and seasonal work — made for meditation, ritual and long thought.

Music is the oldest technology for changing a state of mind without changing a substance in the body. A sustained drone narrows attention; a slow modal line gives it somewhere to walk. These recordings are built for that purpose rather than for the charts — long, patient, and occasionally quite dull if you are not using them properly.

Where a tuning claim is made (432 Hz, 136.1 Hz, binaural beats) we flag it honestly: these are traditions and experiments, not established physiology. Use what works for you and discard the rest.
