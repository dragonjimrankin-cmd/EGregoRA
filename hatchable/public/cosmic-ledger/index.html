The Cosmic Ledger & Phased Integration

Why anything has a value at all. The order's reading is that constants are balances rather than givens — residue left by what has already been integrated — and that the three great fractions of the universe are a reading taken at our own point on the clock. The cosmology comes first and is standard. The doctrine comes second and is marked on every paragraph.

What This Page Is

Everything on this page labelled as measurement is standard cosmology, and you can check all of it against the Planck 2018 results. Everything labelled as the order's teaching is the order's teaching: a candidate account, filed where candidate accounts belong, and not to be cited as physics by anyone, including us.

The teaching has a name and an author. It is part of the Two Infinities Framework, the independent research project of James Rankin, co-founder of this order, which holds three limbs together: the Moebius Seam, the Cosmic Ledger set out here, and the Rankin Skeletal Splice, the numerical work on the ways the Riemann Hypothesis is equivalent. The Framework is soon to be published in full, where these ideas are detailed properly and the mathematics is rigorously tested. Until that publication, what is written here is a summary in plain words, and it is graded as myth and speculation because that is what an unpublished framework is. ◆

The claim, in one breath: collapse leaves a residue; residue is polarity; polarity accumulated over the history of the universe is what gives constants and boson couplings the particular values they have; the residue cannot be quantised, because the act of measurement destroys exactly the record that quantising it would require; and the proportions of dark energy, dark matter and ordinary matter that we measure are the running balance of that ledger at 13.8 billion years — our reading, at our moment, not a fixed property of the world.

Grading throughout: ◆ established science · ◆ scholarship and history · ◆ speculative and contested · ◆ myth, tradition and primary esoteric text.

The Measured Account

The Three Fractions, and How They Are Known

Before any doctrine: here is what is actually measured, how, and how well. The numbers below are the Planck 2018 best fit to the cosmic microwave background, with baryon acoustic oscillations and supernovae in agreement.

How each one is weighed

  • ◆ Ordinary matter from the relative heights of the odd and even peaks in the CMB power spectrum, and independently from the deuterium abundance left over from the first three minutes. Two completely different methods, same answer.
  • ◆ Dark matter from the position and height of the third peak, from galaxy rotation curves, from cluster lensing, and from the Bullet Cluster, where the mass and the gas are visibly in different places.
  • ◆ Dark energy from the dimming of distant type Ia supernovae, from the flat geometry the CMB demands, and from the baryon acoustic scale.

What is honestly unresolved

  • ◆ Nobody knows what dark matter is. Every direct-detection experiment so far has come back empty.
  • ◆ Nobody knows what dark energy is. The cosmological constant fits, and the vacuum-energy calculation that should produce it overshoots by up to 120 orders of magnitude.
  • ◆ The Hubble tension. Early-universe and late-universe measurements of the expansion rate disagree at around five sigma, and nobody has closed it.
  • ◆ DESI 2024–25 reports a mild preference for dark energy that weakens with time. Mild. Watch it; do not spend it.

The Ledger

What Holds Up, and What Does Not

Survives

  • ◆ The three fractions, and their error bars. 68.9 / 26.2 / 4.9 at the present epoch, from independent methods that agree.
  • ◆ The mixture changes with time. Radiation, matter and the cosmological constant dilute as a⁻⁴, a⁻³ and not at all. No doctrine needed for this; it is the expansion.
  • ◆ The coincidence problem is real and open. Saying so is not a concession to mysticism; it is the state of the field.
  • ◆ Couplings run. Measured at colliders, and the running is predicted by the Standard Model to high precision.
  • ◆ Measurement disturbs; information is physical. No-cloning, the Heisenberg relations and Landauer's limit are all real, and all genuinely limit what can be recovered about a past collapse.

Does Not Survive

  • ◆ "Constants are accumulated residue." Every measurement to date says the constants have not moved: |α̇/α| below 10⁻¹⁸ per year. A framework that needs drift must explain why none is seen.
  • ◆ "The ledger sets 68.9 / 26.2 / 4.9." No number has yet been derived from it. Until one is, the mapping is a picture laid over an answer obtained elsewhere.
  • ◆ "It cannot be quantised, therefore it is real." Unmeasurability is not evidence. A quantity defined so that no experiment can touch it is also a quantity no experiment can support.
  • ◆ Dark matter as unphased residue. Dark matter clusters, lenses and falls like cold, collisionless mass. Any story about it must reproduce that behaviour in detail, and this one does not yet try.
  • ◆ "Collapse requires an observer." It does not, and the Ledger must be stated in terms of decoherence rather than observation if it is to survive its first referee.

The Words

The Cosmic Ledger
The Framework's name for the running total of residue left by every collapse in the history of the universe. Readable only as a balance.
Residue polarisation
The directional trace a collapse is held to leave behind when one outcome is kept and the rest are not.
Phased integration
The teaching that matter and energy stand at levels of integration, and that moving between them is what a phase change is.
Scale factor
The single number a describing how much space has stretched. Densities fall as powers of it, which is why the mixture changes.
Ω (omega)
A component's density as a fraction of the critical density. The three fractions on this page are omegas.
Coincidence problem
The unresolved question of why we happen to live in the narrow window when matter and dark energy are comparable.
Running coupling
The measured fact that interaction strengths depend on the energy at which they are probed.
No-cloning
The theorem that an unknown quantum state cannot be copied. One of the real limits the Ledger leans on.
The Two Infinities Framework
James Rankin's independent research project: the Moebius Seam, the Cosmic Ledger and the Rankin Skeletal Splice as one account, soon to be published in full.
Rankin Skeletal Splice
The numerical component of the Framework, claiming new ways in which the Riemann Hypothesis is equivalent.

A Closing Stance

A universe in which the constants are balances rather than decrees is a universe with a history that matters, and the order finds that idea beautiful. Beauty is not evidence. The measured part of this page — the three fractions, their motion, the leash on the constants — would stand if the teaching were abandoned tomorrow, and the teaching knows it.

What would change our minds, in either direction, is a number: a derivation that lands on 26.2 rather than describing it, or a measurement of drift where the clocks currently see none. The Two Infinities Framework is the attempt at the first. Until it is published and tested, this page is offered as a stated position rather than a result.
