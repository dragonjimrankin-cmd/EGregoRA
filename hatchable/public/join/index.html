The Threshold

Join the Order

No passwords. An address you can prove is yours, and after that your own face or fingerprint if your device has one. Membership costs nothing, confers no rank, and withholds no teaching — everything on this site stays public. An account simply means the oracle knows who it is talking to.

STEP ONE · YOUR ADDRESS

Sign in, or create an account

One form does both. If the address is new, an account is made; if it is known, you are signed back in. Either way a six-digit code goes to the inbox and nothing is created until you enter it.

Read the gate-word and type it back

Confirming your address also puts you on the order's mailing list — new pages, the podcast, and the occasional long letter from Ed. Not weekly, never sold, and the way off it sits at the foot of every one of them, one click, no reason asked.

STEP TWO · THE SIX DIGITS

Check your inbox

A code is on its way. It lasts fifteen minutes and works once.

INSIDE

Welcome

Age & identity — the studio door

The image and video generators are the one part of this site kept behind a check. Everything else is open to everyone, signed in or not. There are two ways through: a look at your face, which takes seconds, or a document, which always works.

THE QUICK WAY · A LOOK AT YOUR FACE

Let the camera see you for a moment and a published face model estimates your age. The estimate is computed inside this page — the model is fetched once and run in your browser, so the frames are read where they are taken. Seven readings are averaged, the median is used, and no face print, template or biometric of any kind is kept. If the reading lands anywhere near eighteen the studio asks for a document instead: estimation is only good to a few years, so the line is drawn at twenty-five.

The first scan downloads about a megabyte of model weights, so give it a moment. If your browser blocks the download, or the camera, the document check below always works.

Face a window if you can. Fill the oval with your head, take off sunglasses, and look straight at the lens.

This browser will not give a page camera access, so the document check below is your way through.

or show a document

A flat, well-lit shot of the page carrying your photograph and your date of birth. JPEG, PNG, WebP or HEIC, under 6 MB. You may cover the document number with your thumb — we do not need it.

What we do with it: the photograph is stored privately in this project's own storage, is never published, never shown on the site, and is never given a public link. It is held so that a false declaration leaves evidence, and for no other purpose. The check is honest about what it is — a signed declaration with a document attached, not a forensic examination — and that is what stands behind every image and clip made here.

Biometric entry

Add a passkey and your device will let you back in with Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello, an Android fingerprint or a hardware key. The scan happens on your device and stays there: nothing biometric is sent here, ever. All this server keeps is a public key and the device's word — cryptographically signed — that it checked a real human.

    This browser or device does not offer platform biometrics, so the emailed code remains your way in. Nothing is lost by that.

    What an account is for

    • The oracle greets you by name and keeps the thread of what you have asked.
    • Files you hand it stay attached to you rather than to a browser tab.
    • Images and clips you make are gathered in one place instead of scattering.
    • Nothing on this site is hidden behind it. There are no grades, no fees and no secrets withheld from the page — an account is a convenience, not an initiation.

    How the door is built

    • ◆ No passwords. Nothing to reuse, leak, phish or reset. Proof of the address is a one-time code that expires in fifteen minutes.
    • ◆ Passkeys, not fingerprints. WebAuthn keeps the biometric on your device's secure element; the server only ever sees a public key and a signature.
    • ◆ User verification required. We reject any sign-in where the device did not actually check a face, a finger or a PIN.
    • ◆ Sessions expire. Thirty days, revocable by signing out, and every passkey can be forgotten individually.
    • ◆ We will not sell the list, because there is nothing we want to sell it for.
