The Open Window

Live Cam

When the order has something worth watching as it happens — a working, a build, a conversation, a sky — it opens a window here. Members with the watchword for that feed can look through it.

Two locks, and they are different locks. You need an account on this site, because a feed is not for the street; and you need the watchword for that particular feed, which is set fresh each time and given out by whoever is broadcasting. An account is not an invitation, and a shared word is not an identity, so both are asked for.

How it works, since you will notice: there is no media server behind this site. The camera records two seconds at a time and your browser plays those segments as they land, which puts you a handful of seconds behind the room. It is a real window, just a slightly slow one — and it is honest about that rather than pretending to be a broadcast truck.

The watchword for

Which elemental phase is your integral aligned with?

Asked of everyone at the door. It is not a test and there is no wrong answer — it is how the room is read, and how the broadcaster sees who is in it. Answer as you are today, not as you were.

Two locks, and only two: a member account with a confirmed email address, and the watchword above. No identity or age check is asked of anyone just to watch or to talk — that is only for raising a camera of your own. Not a member yet? Join the order; it takes a minute.

The Room

Everyone in the room is below, their camera if they have one up and their elemental phase if they have not. Press any of them to bring them up large; press again, or “back to the wall”, to let them go.

    Who is in the room

    A row for every person, a column for the elemental phase they declared at the door, and a switch for each power they hold. It refreshes every two and a half seconds, and keeps six rows standing when the room is empty so the controls never move under your hand. Blocking takes chat, camera and microphone at once, so a nuisance is one press rather than three.
