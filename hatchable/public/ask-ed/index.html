Questions Already in the Pile

Is astrology real?

Real as a symbolic system with four millennia of continuous use and enormous cultural weight — yes, demonstrably. Real as a physical force by which Saturn's position alters your week — no; every controlled test of that claim has failed. Both halves of that sentence matter.

Did the Nazis really practise occultism?

Partly, and it has been wildly exaggerated. Himmler's Ahnenerbe genuinely funded pseudo-archaeology and runic mysticism; Hitler was mostly contemptuous of it. The important lesson is not that occultism caused fascism — it is that fascism will loot any symbolic system it can reach, and that symbols have no immune system of their own. We supply the immune system.

Does the Law of One hold up?

As cosmology, it is untestable. As philosophy, its core — that consciousness is unitary and that free will is sacred — sits in good company with Advaita Vedanta, Plotinus and Whitehead. Read it as a text, not a transcript, and it becomes much more interesting.
