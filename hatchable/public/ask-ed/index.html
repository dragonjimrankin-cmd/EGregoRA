The Open Door

Ask Ed a Question

No question is too large, too small or too strange. Several are too urgent — see the note below.

The Oracle

Ask me anything inside the eleven limbs — or anything else at all. Cooking, grief, a gearbox, a bad week, a line of code: I will talk about it properly rather than steering you back to sacred geometry, and I will say I don't know when I don't. If a question needs today's news I will go and look it up and show you where I went, I will draw you a picture if you ask, and you can hand me a file to read and work from.

Gink

Gink is the order's familiar, and he keeps its answers. Ask him here and he will read the reply aloud in his own voice; the words are the order's written ones, unchanged.

He was Ed's fox. He died; the skull stayed, and sits on the desk within reach — a charm that feeds the connections and leans on him like an older part of his own soul. That the bone is there is fact; what it does is Ed's testimony, and we file it as such.

    ASK GINK TO DRAW

    Make an Image

    Type what you want to see and Gink will draw it — photorealistic unless you ask for a painting, an engraving, a diagram or a cartoon. Made with FLUX.1-schnell, an open-weights model, so no company's key is required. Be specific: subject, setting, light, weather, lens, mood. A picture takes fifteen to forty seconds.

    Every image here is generated, not photographed. It is a synthesis made to order and is never evidence of anything. Gink will not draw a real person in a situation they were never in, and nothing made here should be passed off as a record.

    ASK GINK TO FILM

    Make a Video

    Five seconds of 480p video from a sentence, made with HunyuanVideo 1.5 — Tencent's open-weights model, 8.3 billion parameters, code and weights published. Describe motion: what moves, how the camera moves, the light. If the order's own GPU is doing the work the clip takes twenty minutes or more, because the model is fetched fresh each time; on a paid endpoint it is about three. Either way, leave the page open and it will appear below when it is done.

    Tencent, 8.3B. The house default: the best motion of the bunch.

    Every clip is generated, not filmed: nothing here records a real event, and it should never be presented as though it did. Video needs a GPU somewhere even when the model is open — if no video key is connected to this project, Gink will say so rather than pretend.

    THE CUTTING ROOM

    Make a Montage

    Every clip and every picture you generate on this page is collected here. Put them in order, trim the ends or set how long a still holds, choose a cut or a crossfade, and the page will edit them into one film — rendered in your own browser, with no upload and no service in the middle. The result downloads as a single file. Clips made before you cleared the page are not kept; this is a cutting room, not an archive.

    • No shots yet. Draw a picture or film a clip above and it lands here — then any shot can be continued from, so the next one keeps the same characters.

    The edit happens frame by frame on a canvas in this page and is recorded as it plays, so a two-minute montage takes two minutes to make. Nothing is sent anywhere. For anything more ambitious — narration, music, titles, stock footage, a real timeline — OpenMontage is installed in this repository under openmontage/; see the README there.

    Questions Already in the Pile

    Is astrology real?

    Real as a symbolic system with four millennia of continuous use and enormous cultural weight — yes, demonstrably. Real as a physical force by which Saturn's position alters your week — no; every controlled test of that claim has failed. Both halves of that sentence matter.

    Does the Law of One hold up?

    As cosmology, it is untestable. As philosophy, its core — that consciousness is unitary and that free will is sacred — sits in good company with Advaita Vedanta, Plotinus and Whitehead. Read it as a text, not a transcript, and it becomes much more interesting.
