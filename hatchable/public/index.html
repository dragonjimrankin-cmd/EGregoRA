The Charter

What an Egregore Is — and Why We Named Ourselves For One

An egregore is the old name for a thought-form grown large: the living spirit that forms when many minds attend to the same idea for long enough that it begins to attend back. A grove, a guild, a nation, a religion, a scientific paradigm — each is an egregore, fed by attention and starved by neglect. EGregoRA is one deliberately built in daylight, with its hands open: EG for its founder Edward Gregory, RA for the old solar name that keeps turning up wherever people go looking for the source.

We take the position that the division between physics and philosophy, between laboratory and grove, is an administrative convenience of the last four centuries, not a feature of the universe. The electron does not know it belongs to a department. Neither does the oak, the mycelium under it, the serotonin receptor, or the strange recursive feeling of being someone reading this sentence.

“Study the stars with a telescope, a calendar, a myth and a nervous system. Four instruments, one sky. Discard whichever of them lies to you — but find out first which one it was.” Edward Gregory, Founder

The Eight Limbs

Fields of Enquiry

Eight doorways into one room.

Limb I

Cosmic Physics

Relativity, quantum field theory, thermodynamics and cosmology read as cosmology once was: an account of what the whole thing is doing and what that implies about us. Entropy, horizons, information, the arrow of time.

Limb II

Druidry, Trees & the Living Earth

The grove as a laboratory. Phenology, mycorrhizal networks, the ogham tree alphabet, the eight stations of the solar year, and what it does to a nervous system to keep seasonal time for a decade.

Limb III

Sacred Geometry & Natural Form

Phyllotaxis, the Fibonacci spiral, close-packing, minimal surfaces, Platonic and Archimedean solids — why pattern recurs across scales, and where the honest mathematics ends and the wishful numerology begins.

Limb IV

Astrology as Symbolic Technology

Not as a prediction engine but as humanity's oldest symbolic operating system: a 4,000-year archive of how we map character, time and meaning onto the sky. Studied historically, used reflectively, tested honestly.

Limb V

Neuroscience, Psychology & the Visionary State

Predictive processing, the default mode network, entropic brain theory; meditation, breath, fasting and the serotonergic psychedelics as instruments that alter the observer rather than the observed.

Limb VI

Biology & the Stubbornness of Life

Autopoiesis, symbiogenesis, morphogenesis, bioelectricity. How matter learned to hold a shape against the current, and why every cell is already solving problems we have no good language for.

Limb VII

God, the Law of One & the Contact Question

Non-duality across traditions; the Ra material read as a philosophical text rather than scripture; Fermi, Drake, Avi Loeb, the UAP record and the discipline of holding a question open without filling it.

Limb VIII

The Shadow Cabinet

How esoteric ideas get captured by tyranny — the Thule and Ahnenerbe occultism of the Nazi regime, the theft of ancient symbols, the pseudo-science of race. Studied as pathology, named as atrocity, and never as inspiration.

Limb IX

Magic & the Wizard's Craft

Magic defined operationally: the deliberate use of symbol, attention and ritual to reconfigure a mind, and through that mind, a life. Ceremony, sigil, memory palace, intention. The wizard as applied psychologist with better robes.

✦ ✦ ✦

On the Shadow Cabinet. EGregoRA studies the Nazi appropriation of myth, symbol and pseudo-science for one reason only: because the twentieth century proved that mystical language is flammable, and that people who never learn how it burns are the easiest to set alight. Nothing in this order romanticises fascism. We name it for what it was — an industrial atrocity dressed in stolen runes — and we teach the warning signs so that the symbols can be returned to the people they were taken from.

The Founders

Edward Gregory & Jim Rankin

Edward is a natural philosopher in the old, unembarrassed sense of the phrase: someone who refuses to choose between the telescope and the tree. He founded EGregoRA to build the thing he could never find — a place where cosmology, ecology, neuroscience and the esoteric traditions could be argued about in the same room, by the same person, without anyone having to pretend.

Jim Rankin is co-founder of the order — Ed's understudy, and on the better days his muse. Where Ed presses a question until it yields, Jim is the one who asks why that question and not another; a good deal of what EGregoRA now calls its own began as something Jim said sideways and Ed refused to let go of. The two halves of the work, the pursuit and the provocation.

Their standing instruction to every member is three words long: test everything kindly.
