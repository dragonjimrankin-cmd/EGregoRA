The Marks

Four Logos, and What Each One Argues

A mark is a compressed argument. These four are drawn as vector art — they are live SVG on this page, not pictures of logos — so they go on a seal, a hoodie or a billboard without losing a hairline.

The Lines We Print

  • “Life, Love, Magic.”the creed, and the only three words on the plain goods
  • “Test everything kindly.”the house rule, and the mug
  • “An order of enquiry.”the tote, the letterhead, the gate
  • “Name the thing accurately. That is the first act of magic.”the codex, page one
  • “The aether was never disproved. One clause of it was struck out.”the physics print
  • “Four instruments, one sky.”the astrolabe engraving
  • “An answer that cannot survive your scepticism has not earned your belief.”the reading-room poster
  • “Roots repeat branches.”inside the collar of every garment

Every line above is the order’s own, taken from its pages rather than bought in. Nothing here quotes a living person without saying so.
