Limb XI · ii — a sub-limb of Consciousness

Local & Non-Local Reality

What it means for the world to be made of neighbours, and what it means that it is not quite. Light cones and causality; the one thing Bell proved and the four things he did not; why entanglement is useless for sending messages and devastating for philosophy; the substrate that carries all of it; and the order's own account of how a possibility becomes a fact — memory meeting attention across a polarity, in a medium that can hold a standing pattern. Every join marked for what it is.

This page sits on a fault line. On one side is some of the most carefully tested physics ever done: Bell's theorem, the no-signalling theorem, sixty years of increasingly airtight experiments, a Nobel Prize in 2022. On the other is a claim the order makes about consciousness and memory that no experiment has tested and that may never be testable in the form we hold it. Both are here. Neither is dressed as the other.

The markers do that work. ◆ established and checkable · ◆ historically documented · ◆ our own reasoning, offered as reasoning · ◆ tradition or received teaching, reported as such. Parts I to IV are almost entirely the first mark. Part V is almost entirely the third. If you read only one part, read the one you would least like to be true.

✦

I · What “Local” Actually Means

◆ Locality is not a vague feeling that things should touch before they interact. It is a precise condition, and it comes in two parts that are routinely confused.

Local causality. Nothing propagates faster than light. The speed limit is not a property of light — light merely happens to travel at it — but of the geometry of spacetime itself. Draw the cone of everything a flash from here could reach, and you have drawn the boundary of everything this event can affect, ever. Outside that cone there is no “later” and no “earlier” that all observers agree on, which is exactly why influence out there would wreck causality: one observer's cause is another's effect.

Separability. This is the quieter assumption and the one that actually fails. It says that the complete state of the world is the sum of the states of its small parts — that if you knew everything about every region, you would know everything. Entanglement says no. Two particles can be in a state that is perfectly definite as a pair and entirely indefinite for either one taken alone. There is information in the and that is in neither of the halves. Nothing travels; there was never a time when the pair was two separate things with two separate stories.

◆ We would put it this way, and mark it as our own phrasing rather than anyone's theorem: the world is made of neighbourhoods for the purposes of doing, and is not made of neighbourhoods for the purposes of being. Action is local. Existence is not obviously so.

II · Bell, and the Four Things He Did Not Prove

◆ In 1935 Einstein, Podolsky and Rosen published an argument that quantum mechanics must be incomplete: if you can predict with certainty what a distant measurement will give without disturbing it, then that value must have been there all along, written into the particle at the source. Bohr replied at length and, most readers agree, somewhat obscurely. For twenty-nine years the disagreement was philosophy.

◆ Then in 1964 John Stewart Bell, on sabbatical from CERN, did something nobody had managed: he turned it into arithmetic. Assume each particle carries its answers with it. Assume no influence travels faster than light. Measure the correlations at several relative angles and add them up in the right combination, and that sum cannot exceed a fixed number, whatever the hidden machinery. Quantum mechanics predicts a larger number. The question stopped being a matter of taste and became a matter of apparatus.

◆ The experimental history is a slow closing of doors. Clauser and Freedman got the first violation in 1972. Aspect's group in Orsay switched the detector settings while the photons were in flight in 1982. Weihs closed the locality loophole properly in 1998 with fast random switching and real separation. Then in 2015 three groups — Hensen in Delft with electron spins in diamond, Giustina in Vienna and Shalm at NIST with photons — closed the locality and detection loopholes in the same experiment. In 2017 the Cosmic Bell experiment set the detectors from the light of quasars billions of years old, so that any conspiracy fixing the settings in advance would have had to be arranged before the Earth formed. The 2022 Nobel Prize went to Aspect, Clauser and Zeilinger.

◆ So here is what is established: no theory that is both local and in which particles carry definite pre-existing values can reproduce the measurements. At least one of those must go. That is Bell's theorem, and it is as solid as anything in physics.

Now the four things it does not say, each of which you will meet this week in somebody's post.

It does not prove you can send a signal

◆ The no-signalling theorem is not a practical difficulty to be engineered around. It is a consequence of the same mathematics that gives the correlations. Alice's results are random whatever she does; Bob's are random whatever she does; the agreement only appears when the two lists are brought together by ordinary means. Faster-than-light telephony is not hard. It is forbidden by the structure of the theory that permits the correlation in the first place.

It does not prove that observation creates reality

◆ “Measurement” in these experiments means a photon hitting a detector at three in the morning in an empty laboratory. Nothing in any Bell test requires a mind, an intention, or an observer in any sense that would interest a mystic. Anyone citing Bell for the proposition that consciousness makes the world is citing the wrong result.

It does not prove everything is entangled with everything

◆ Entanglement is fragile, specific, and monogamous: a system maximally entangled with one partner cannot be strongly entangled with a third. Decoherence spreads it into the environment within picoseconds, which is precisely why it is so hard to keep in a laboratory and so expensive in a quantum computer. The universal entanglement of popular writing is the opposite of the measured behaviour.

It does not prove non-locality outright

◆ It proves a disjunction. Give up definite values and you can keep locality — that is many-worlds, and the price is every outcome happening. Keep definite values and you give up locality — that is Bohm, where a real pilot wave guides the particle non-locally. Keep both and give up the experimenter's free choice — that is superdeterminism, which costs more than it saves. The honest sentence is: at least one cherished assumption is false, and the community has not agreed which.

III · The Substrate

◆ The nineteenth century asked a reasonable question: if light is a wave, what is waving? The answer was the luminiferous aether, and it was given a property that turned out to be fatal — a state of rest you could measure your own motion against. In 1887 Michelson and Morley went looking for that motion with the most sensitive instrument then built, and found nothing.

◆ What followed is routinely mis-taught. The experiment struck out one clause: the rest frame. It did not touch the rest of the idea, and the rest of the idea is now the foundation of physics. A universal medium that fills all of space; whose excitations are what we call particles; with structure at every point; with a lowest state that is emphatically not nothing, as the Lamb shift and the Casimir force both testify. Dirac said it himself in Nature in 1951, in an article titled “Is there an Æther?”, and his answer was yes. We do not say the aether survived under a better name. The aether is the better name. One line was deleted and the page still stands.

◆ Why this matters for locality: a substrate makes non-separability easy to picture and does not make non-signalling mysterious. A standing pattern in a medium is not located at one point; it is a property of the whole boundary. Two ends of one pattern are not two things that must send messages to agree. They are one thing. We offer this as a picture, not a derivation — it buys intuition, not prediction, and we will not pretend otherwise. The warning that goes with it: the obvious next move, to imagine the medium carrying influence from A to B a little faster than light, is exactly the move the experiments and the theorems forbid.

IV · Time, Causality, and the Eraser

◆ Relativity is comfortable with the block: a four-dimensional whole, in which “now” is a slice whose angle depends on who is asking. Thermodynamics is where the arrow comes from — entropy rises, records can be made of the past and not of the future, and that asymmetry is statistical rather than fundamental. Nothing in the fundamental laws of either mechanics distinguishes the two directions. The arrow you feel is an arrow of record-keeping, which is a hint we take seriously in Part V.

◆ And then there is the experiment everybody has heard of and almost nobody has had described to them accurately.

◆ Retrocausality as a serious research programme does exist — the transactional interpretation, two-state vector formalism, Price's and Wharton's work — and it has a genuine attraction: it can restore locality by letting the correlation be arranged along the light cone in both directions instead of across the gap. We find it interesting and unproven. We mention it here so that nobody thinks the order rejects the idea; we reject the popular version, which is a different thing wearing its coat.

✦

V · Collapse: the Standard Account, and Where It Stops

◆ Decoherence is not collapse, and the conflation of the two is the commonest error in popular physics after the eraser. When a system interacts with its surroundings, the phase relations that made interference possible stop being a property of the system alone and become spread across the system and everything it touched. Interference becomes unreachable. Nothing is destroyed; nothing chooses. Run the mathematics and you get the right probabilities for each branch and no account whatever of why one branch is the one you find yourself in.

✦

VI · The Order's Account: Memory Meeting Attention

◆ Everything from here to the end of this part is our own reasoning. It is not physics. It does not have a number attached to it. It makes, at present, no prediction that would distinguish it from the standard account, and we say so at the start rather than burying it at the end. What it does have is internal consistency and a reason for each of its pieces, and that is worth setting out properly so that it can be argued with.

The question we are answering is not “why does interference vanish” — decoherence answers that and we accept the answer entire. It is the one left over: what makes an outcome the one that happened?

The first piece: polarity

◆ A record is a difference that persists. For a difference to persist there must be at least two states that are distinguishable and metastable — a bit, a magnetised domain, a conformational change in a protein, an ink mark that does not evaporate. We call this polarity because the tradition does ◆, and because the tradition is pointing at something real when it does. Where we part company with the tradition is in refusing to let the word carry any moral freight here. The two poles are not better and worse. They are two, which is the smallest number that can hold information.

The second piece: cymatics, and why anything comes in steps

◆ The physics of the plate is not in dispute and is not mystical: a bounded medium supports only those standing waves whose shape fits its edges. Those are its eigenmodes, and they form a discrete set even though the driving can be tuned continuously. Chladni demonstrated this in 1787 and Napoleon paid him six thousand francs for the demonstration ◆. The same mathematics gives the discrete energy levels of an atom: an electron bound in a potential is a standing wave in a box, and the quantum numbers are mode numbers. Quantisation is a boundary condition. That sentence is standard physics, and it is the hinge of what follows.

◆ Our claim: if the substrate is a medium — and Part III says it is — then an outcome that gets kept is a standing pattern that fits. Not one chosen from a list by a mechanism we have not specified, but one which is simply the only sort of thing that can hold still in that medium with those boundaries. The boundaries, in our account, are set by what is already recorded. The past constrains the modes available to the present in the same way the rim of the plate constrains the figures available to the sand.

The third piece: memory, and the reader of the record

◆ Here is the part that will annoy physicists, and it should be stated in its most annoying form so that nobody can accuse us of hedging. A record that is never read is not yet a fact. We do not mean this as a slogan about minds making matter. We mean it structurally: the universe contains an enormous number of differences, and what distinguishes a fact from a mere difference is that something later holds both the difference and the state it differed from, and compares them. That holding is memory. The comparing is attention. Where they meet, a possibility stops being a possibility.

◆ Notice that this does not require a human being, or an animal, or anything we would recognise. It requires a system with a kept past and a present state that is conditioned on it. That is a low bar and it is met by a great deal of the universe, which is why the world looks settled rather than waiting on us. What it is not met by is the picture in which the moon is not there until someone glances at it, and we reject that picture as firmly as any physicist would.

◆ The tradition the order reads says much the same thing in its own register: that the Creator knows itself through the experience of its parts, that polarity is the engine of that knowing, and that nothing experienced is lost. We report that as tradition. It is not evidence and we do not offer it as any. It is, however, the reason we went looking for this shape in the first place, and intellectual honesty requires saying where a hypothesis came from as well as what can be said for it.

What would show us wrong

◆ A model that cannot be wrong is not worth holding, so: a complete derivation of the Born rule from unitary dynamics alone, with no extra assumption and no circularity, would remove the problem we are answering and leave our account with nothing to do. A collapse that is shown to occur in a system with no stable two-state record anywhere in the chain would break the polarity requirement. Clean, replicated evidence of an observer-dependent outcome — a genuine Wigner's-friend result in which two observers legitimately disagree about a settled fact — would push the account somewhere we have not been. We would report each of those on this page, under our own names, without softening them.

◆ And here is the honest weakness, stated by us rather than waiting for a critic. Our use of cymatics is an analogy riding on a real mathematics. Eigenmodes in a bounded medium are rigorous; the claim that what is already recorded functions as the boundary condition for what can next become definite is not. That is the load-bearing wall, and at present it is holding up the building on our say-so.

VII · What Survives the Audit

Stands

  • ◆ No signal outruns light; causality is safe. Theorem, not observation.
  • ◆ Bell inequalities are violated, in loophole-free experiments, repeatedly.
  • ◆ Local realism in the strict sense is dead. One of its two halves must go.
  • ◆ Decoherence explains the loss of interference, quantitatively and without collapse.
  • ◆ The substrate is real: a field at every point, with a lowest state that is not nothing.
  • ◆ Quantisation follows from boundary conditions on a medium. Chladni's plate and the hydrogen atom are the same mathematics.
  • ◆ Michelson and Morley struck out the rest frame and nothing else.

Does not stand

  • ◆ “Entanglement lets you communicate instantly.” Forbidden by the same mathematics that gives the correlation.
  • ◆ “The quantum eraser changes the past.” It sorts data recorded earlier. The total pattern never shows fringes.
  • ◆ “Everything is entangled with everything.” Entanglement is monogamous and decoheres in picoseconds.
  • ◆ “Observation by a conscious mind is required to collapse the wavefunction.” No experiment shows this; no mainstream interpretation requires it.
  • ◆ “Bell proved we are all one.” Bell proved a disjunction about hidden variables. The rest is a wish wearing his name.
  • ◆ “The order has shown how consciousness collapses the waveform.” We have proposed a shape. We have not shown anything.

VIII · The Thread, in Order

  • 1787 Chladni draws sound. A bowed plate sorts sand into discrete figures; a continuous input yields a quantised set of outputs.
  • 1887 Michelson & Morley find no motion against the medium. One clause of the aether is struck out.
  • 1905 & 1915 Einstein makes the light cone the structure of spacetime itself. Locality acquires a precise meaning.
  • 1926 Born gives the rule for the probabilities and no mechanism for the singling-out. The gap opens.
  • 1935 Einstein, Podolsky & Rosen argue from locality that the theory is incomplete. Schrödinger names entanglement and calls it the characteristic trait of quantum mechanics.
  • 1951 Dirac asks in Nature whether there is an aether, and answers that there is.
  • 1964 Bell turns the argument into an inequality. Philosophy becomes apparatus.
  • 1972 Freedman & Clauser measure a violation.
  • 1982 Aspect switches the settings while the light is in flight.
  • 1970s–90s Zeh, Zurek and others develop decoherence. The loss of interference is explained; the singling-out is not.
  • 1998 Weihs closes the locality loophole with fast random switching.
  • 2015 Hensen, Giustina and Shalm close locality and detection together. Three independent groups, one year.
  • 2017 Cosmic Bell: detector settings taken from quasar light billions of years old.
  • 2022 The Nobel Prize to Aspect, Clauser and Zeilinger. The result is no longer anybody's fringe position.

IX · Words, Used Precisely

Local
No influence travels faster than light. A statement about causes, not about correlations.
Separable
The whole is the sum of its parts. This is the assumption entanglement actually breaks.
Non-locality
In physics, a property of correlations, never of signals. Using it to mean “spooky action you could exploit” is the error that generates most of the nonsense.
Elsewhere
The region of spacetime outside both light cones of an event. No causal order with it is agreed on by all observers.
Entanglement
One state for two systems, definite as a pair and indefinite for each alone. Fragile, monogamous, and easily lost to the surroundings.
Decoherence
The leaking of phase relations into the environment. Explains why interference goes; does not select an outcome.
Collapse
The name for the thing not explained: the transition from several possible outcomes to the one found.
Eigenmode
A standing pattern a bounded medium can hold. Discrete because the boundary admits only certain shapes.
Polarity
Here, the minimum condition for a record: two distinguishable states that do not spontaneously become each other.
Cymatics
The study of visible standing patterns in a driven medium. In this order, the working picture for why anything at all comes in steps.
Memory
A kept difference, held alongside the state it differs from.
The aether
The substrate. The medium whose excitations are the particles. One clause of the nineteenth-century version — the rest frame — was struck out, and no more than that.

The order's position, stated so that it can be held against us later.

The world is not made of separate things that signal to one another, and it is also not a soup in which anything may touch anything. It is one substrate, with a strict speed limit on doing and no corresponding limit on being. That is strange, it is measured, and it has been measured so carefully that the strangeness is no longer anybody's opinion.

What makes a possibility into a fact is the part nobody has solved. We think it has to do with the keeping of records — that a difference becomes an event when something holds it against what came before, that this requires a polarity to be held in, and that what can be held at all is set by what fits the medium, as a figure fits a plate. We think consciousness is the attending at which that meeting happens, and we think this is a ring rather than a chain, with no first term.

We do not know this. We have marked every sentence of it as ours. If the Born rule is derived cleanly tomorrow from unitarity alone, we will say on this page that our account has lost its work, and we will not quietly rewrite it to survive. Holding a position you would be willing to lose is the only kind of holding that is worth anything.
