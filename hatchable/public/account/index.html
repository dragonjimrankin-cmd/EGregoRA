    No one is signed in on this device

    An account is a convenience, never a gate on the teaching: every page and every answer here stays open whether you hold one or not. It exists so the oracle knows your name, keeps your thread, and can open the image and video studio to you once your age is checked.

    What is held, exactly

    • Your email address, and the name you chose to be called by.
    • A session token per device, good for thirty days, revocable here.
    • A public key for each passkey — never a fingerprint, never a face.
    • If you passed the document check: your declared legal name, date of birth and the photograph you supplied, stored privately and never published.
    • If you passed by face scan: the estimated number, and one frame only where the reading was taken in your browser and nothing else witnessed it.

    Leaving

    Signing out ends this device's session at once. If you want the account and everything attached to it erased — address, documents, letters, the lot — write to info@shakra.co.uk from the address on file and it will be done, without an argument and without a retention offer.

    We would rather have ten people who want to be here than a thousand who cannot find the door out.
