For Ed — adding a plate. Drop an SVG or PNG into src/assets/img/ and add a <figure class="frame"> block to src/infographics.njk. SVG is strongly preferred: it stays crisp at any zoom, weighs almost nothing, and the text inside it is searchable and screen-readable.
