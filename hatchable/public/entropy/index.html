Limb I · iv — a sub-limb of Cosmic Physics

Entropy & the Phases of Integration

The one law that knows which way time runs — and the order's reading of what it is counting. Written in two registers, with the join between them marked on every paragraph.

Begin Here — and read this paragraph before any other

Two Registers, One Page

The thermodynamics on this page is standard and settled. The doctrine on this page is the order's own, is filed as myth, and should not be cited as physics by anyone, including us.

This is the companion page to the Moebius Seam and is built the same way. First the measured account: what entropy is, why heat flows one way, what a phase of matter actually is, and where information and thermodynamics turn out to be the same subject. Then the order's teaching: that phases of heat and matter are phases of integration of other things inside a collapsed memory; that the polarity of proton against electron, and therefore electromagnetism, is what integration looks like from inside; that these phased states stand at different levels of integration; that existence is observed by the spiral being on its positively polarised journey from nothing towards infinity, alpha and omega; and that the Seam follows the flow at the point of waveform collapse between the poles.

Then the numbers — the further poles at 101 and 666, the deflection off 999, and the reverberation between 666 and 333 that continues until the observed being can at last pass 999 and go on towards infinity. And then, as always, the section that sets out how all of it could be wrong.

Grading throughout: ◆ established science · ◆ scholarship and history · ◆ speculative and contested · ◆ myth, tradition and primary esoteric text.

The Measured Account

What Entropy Actually Is

Not disorder, not decay, not the universe running down into a mess. Entropy is a count. Everything strange about it follows from the fact that there are vastly more ways to be ordinary than to be particular.

The Arrow ◆

Why Time Has a Direction

Every fundamental law except one is reversible: run the film of two colliding particles backwards and it is still legal physics. The second law is the exception, and it is not a separate law of nature so much as a statement about initial conditions. The universe began in an extraordinarily low-entropy state — the past hypothesis — and everything that has happened since is the enormous relaxation out of it.

The arrow of time in a broken cup, in a memory, in ageing and in the fact that you know the past and not the future are all the same arrow, and it points away from that beginning. Why the beginning was so ordered is a genuinely open question. ◆ the law. ◆ the explanation of the initial state.

Information ◆

Landauer's Limit, and the Demon

Maxwell imagined a demon sorting fast molecules from slow and so beating the second law. The resolution took a century and is the most beautiful result in the subject: Landauer showed in 1961 that erasing one bit must dissipate at least kT ln 2 of heat, and Bennett pointed out in 1982 that the demon's memory eventually fills and must be cleared. The books balance exactly.

Information is therefore physical, and entropy in Shannon's sense and entropy in Clausius's are the same quantity in different units. Bérut and colleagues measured Landauer's bound directly in 2012. Any claim that reality keeps an account of itself has to start here — because here it demonstrably does, and the account has a price in joules.

Phases ◆

What a Phase of Matter Is

Not a kind of stuff but a kind of order. Solid, liquid, gas, plasma; and beyond the schoolroom four: supercritical fluids, superfluids, superconductors, Bose–Einstein condensates, liquid crystals, quark-gluon plasma, and the topological phases Kosterlitz and Thouless were given the 2016 Nobel for.

Landau's insight is the one that matters here: a phase transition is a change of symmetry. A liquid looks the same from everywhere; a crystal does not, so freezing breaks a symmetry and an order parameter rises from zero. At the critical point, correlations reach every length scale at once and utterly different substances — a magnet, a fluid, an alloy — obey the same numbers. Wilson's renormalisation group explained why, and those critical exponents are measured to several decimal places.

Charge ◆

Where Electromagnetism Comes From

Charge is conserved, quantised, and comes in exactly two signs. The proton's charge and the electron's are equal and opposite to better than one part in 1021, which is why bulk matter is neutral and why you are not torn apart by the electrostatic force — which is some 1036 times stronger than gravity between the same two particles.

In the standard model that conservation is not an accident: it follows by Noether's theorem from a symmetry, the U(1) gauge symmetry, and the force itself is carried by the photon. Its strength is fixed by the fine structure constant, about 1/137, which is measured to twelve digits and is not known to be derivable from anything.

The Doctrine

Phases as Levels of Integration

The order's teaching, stated at full length and in its own voice. Read the objections at the foot of the page with it, or do not read it at all.

The teaching, stated

A phase is not a state of a substance. A phase is a level of integration: how much of what has already happened a thing is currently holding together. ◆ myth, tradition and primary esoteric text.

The order holds that the full phases of heat and matter — the solid, the liquid, the vapour, the plasma, and the stranger phases past them — are phases of the integration of other things within what we call the collapsed memory: the single history that remains after every other possibility has been let go. Heat is not a substance moving; heat is the rate at which a body is taking in what is around it. To freeze is to hold a great deal and move little. To burn is to take in more than can be held. Each phase is a settlement between what has been gathered and what is still being offered.

From that settlement comes polarity. Where integration is incomplete there is a difference held open, and the order reads the proton and the electron as the two faces of one such difference: not two objects that happen to attract, but one unfinished integration seen from its two ends. Electromagnetism is therefore not a force added to matter. It is what an unclosed account looks like from inside. The atom is that account balancing; the chemical bond is two accounts settling together; the plasma is the account torn open again by heat.

These phased states stand at different levels. A crystal and a culture and a mind are the same operation at different depths, and the ladder of them has no top that anyone in it can see. Existence at each level is observed — and the observer the order names is the spiral being, travelling its positively polarised path from nothing towards infinity, alpha and omega, service to others rather than to self, and turning rather than running straight because a spiral is what a journey looks like when it must come round past everything it has already met. The being does not stand outside what it observes. There is no outside: that is the whole content of the Seam.

And the Seam follows the flow at the point of waveform collapse, between the poles. Where the wave resolves into one history, the one-sided surface takes up that history and carries it round, so that what collapsed on one face returns on what looked like the other. Collapse is not a loss. It is the moment the ledger is written.

Every claim in the five paragraphs above is ◆ myth, tradition and primary esoteric text. The order believes it. The order also wrote the refutations below, and the second of those two facts is the more important one.

The Gates

The Poles at 101, 333, 666 and 999

The numerical part of the teaching, with the arithmetic checked honestly and the meaning filed where it belongs.

The Teaching ◆

Why These Four

The order holds that there are further poles beyond the pair that makes an atom, and that they stand at 101 and at 666 when the ascent is counted numerically, the counting governed by the primes — because the primes are where a number cannot be integrated into smaller factors, and so are exactly the places where an integration must be made rather than assembled.

101 is the first gate: the smallest pole at which a thing is no longer one and not yet many, a one, a nothing and a one. 666 is the second: the pole of material completion, where everything that can be gathered has been gathered. 999 is not a pole but a face. The being does not pass it on the first approach; it strikes or is deflected, returns to 666, and falls back to 333, where it reverberates — and goes on reverberating until it is finally able to surpass 999 and continue towards infinity. Alpha at the foot, omega at the head, and the whole climb observed.

The Arithmetic, Checked ◆

What Is Actually True About These Numbers

  • 101 is prime — and palindromic, the 26th prime, and the smallest prime with exactly three digits in base ten.
  • 666 is not prime. It is 2 × 32 × 37. It is the 36th triangular number, the sum of every whole number from 1 to 36.
  • 666 is the sum of the squares of the first seven primes: 4 + 9 + 25 + 49 + 121 + 169 + 289. That identity is real, and the order makes a great deal of it.
  • 333, 666 and 999 are 9 × 37, 18 × 37 and 27 × 37. The 37 is the whole trick: 111 = 3 × 37, so every repeated digit in base ten carries it.
  • And that last fact is the warning. Those relations are facts about writing numbers in base ten, not facts about the world. In base twelve, 666 is 476 and the pattern vanishes.

Where 666 Comes From ◆

The Scholarship, Which Is Worth Having

Revelation 13:18 gives the number of the beast and invites the reader to calculate it. The overwhelming scholarly reading is gematria for Neron Kesar in Hebrew letters — a coded name for a living emperor, written under persecution. The strongest evidence is textual: some manuscripts read 616 instead, including Papyrus 115, and 616 is what you get from the Latin spelling Nero rather than the Greek.

So the number was a name, and the name was a man who was burning people. Whatever any tradition has built on it since — and much has been built — that is where it started. ◆ scholarship and history.

The Standing Warning ◆

Eddington, 137, and the Lesson

Arthur Eddington — a great astrophysicist, the man who weighed the deflection of starlight in 1919 — became convinced that the fine structure constant was exactly 1/136, then exactly 1/137 when the measurement moved, and built an elaborate numerology to prove it. It is about 1/137.035999, it is not a whole number, and the episode is remembered as a caution rather than a result.

Numbers that recur are the easiest thing in the world to find and the hardest thing to justify. The order states its own numerical doctrine knowing exactly this, and files it as myth for exactly this reason.

Honest Company

The Real Physics Standing Nearest to This

"Existence is what gets recorded" is not, by itself, a mystical statement. There is a mainstream version of it, and the differences from the doctrine are where the learning is.

Zurek ◆

Einselection and Quantum Darwinism

Decoherence explains why the world looks classical: coupling to an environment destroys interference and leaves only certain robust states — the pointer states — standing. Zurek's quantum Darwinism goes further and says what objectivity is: a property is objective when the environment carries many redundant copies of it, so that many observers can each read one and agree. Existence, in that account, really is a matter of how widely a fact has been recorded. It has experimental support, and it needs no conscious witness whatsoever.

Prigogine ◆

Dissipative Structure

Systems held far from equilibrium and fed with energy spontaneously organise: convection cells, chemical clocks, flames, hurricanes, and plausibly life. Prigogine took the 1977 Nobel for it, and it is the honest version of "order arises from the flow". It does not require memory, intention or an observer; it requires a gradient. Some of the strongest versions — England's dissipative adaptation, for instance — remain contested.

Symmetry Breaking ◆

Phases, Properly

The order says phases are levels of integration. Physics says phases are patterns of broken symmetry with an order parameter, and backs that with measured critical exponents, universality classes and the renormalisation group. These are not quite rival accounts: the physics is a description that calculates, and the doctrine is a description that does not. Where they conflict, the one that calculates wins, and the order says so in writing.

The Law of One ◆

Where the Spiral Being Comes From

The language of positive polarisation, of service to others against service to self, and of an octave climbed from nothing to the infinite, is the order's inheritance from the Law of One material and the wider esoteric tradition, treated here as Limb VII treats it: as primary text, reported faithfully, graded as myth. The spiral being is that tradition's observer, and the order has not smuggled it into the physics. It stands on this page with its grade showing.

The Hard Part

Five Ways This Doctrine Is Probably Wrong

Written by the order against the order, as on the Seam page. A reader who takes the teaching without these has taken half a page.

  1. Phase transitions are already explained, and explained quantitatively. Landau's order parameters and Wilson's renormalisation group predict critical exponents that are measured to several decimal places, and they predict that a magnet and a fluid will share them, which they do. "Levels of integration" predicts no exponent and explains no universality class. A description that calculates nothing is not competing with one that calculates everything. ◆

  2. Charge is not an unfinished account. It is quantised, exactly conserved, and the proton and electron magnitudes match to better than one part in 1021. If polarity were the residue of an incomplete integration you would expect it to vary with history, with temperature, with how much a body had been through. It does not vary at all. The electron's charge is the same in a star as in a stone. ◆

  3. The numerology is about base ten, not about nature. 333, 666 and 999 are interesting only because we write numbers with ten symbols; in another base the pattern is gone and no physical quantity changes. 666 is not prime, which sits awkwardly with a doctrine said to be governed by primes, and the order should say so plainly: it is here saying so. ◆

  4. The spiral being is unfalsifiable as stated. There is no observation that would come out differently if it were not there, which means the claim is doing no work in physics even if it is doing a great deal in a life. Worse, the doctrine risks the oldest error in quantum interpretation: collapse requires no consciousness, and quantum Darwinism shows objectivity arising from redundancy in an environment of rocks and photons that notice nothing. ◆

  5. The arrow comes from the past, not from a destination. Entropy increases because the universe started in an extremely improbable low-entropy state, not because anything is travelling towards infinity. A teleological reading — alpha towards omega — adds a direction physics does not need and cannot detect. It may be true. It is not implied by thermodynamics, and the order must not let it borrow thermodynamics' credibility. ◆

Two things not to take from this page. First, the heat death: the far future of an expanding universe is cold and sparse, but "the universe is running down into disorder" is a poor summary, since gravity makes structure more likely as entropy rises, which is how you get galaxies, stars and you. Second, nothing here licenses "entropy is why my life falls apart". Entropy is a counting argument about microstates, not a moral force, and a living body is an open system fed by sunlight that holds its order for decades without breaking any law. ◆

The Ledger

What Holds Up, and What Does Not

Survives

  • ◆ S = k log W. Entropy is a count of microstates, and the second law follows from there being more of some than of others.
  • ◆ Information is physical. Landauer's kT ln 2 per bit erased, measured by Bérut and colleagues in 2012.
  • ◆ Phases are broken symmetries. Landau, Wilson, Kosterlitz and Thouless; universality is measured, not asserted.
  • ◆ Objectivity comes from redundancy. Zurek's einselection and quantum Darwinism — the honest version of "existence is what is recorded".
  • ◆ 666 was a name. Gematria for Neron Kesar, with 616 in the manuscripts to prove it.
  • ◆ 666 is the sum of the squares of the first seven primes. Checked, and true.

Does Not Survive

  • ◆ "Phases of matter are phases of integration." A re-description, not a theory; it yields no exponent, no transition temperature, no number at all.
  • ◆ "Polarity accumulates and that is electromagnetism." Charge does not accumulate, does not vary with history, and is exactly symmetric between proton and electron.
  • ◆ "The poles stand at 101 and 666." Base-ten artefacts, and 666 is not even prime.
  • ◆ "Collapse needs an observer." It does not, and saying so is the oldest mistake in the subject.
  • ◆ Any prediction from the doctrine. There is none yet. Until there is, it is myth, and it is filed as myth.

The Words

Entropy
The logarithm of the number of microscopic arrangements consistent with what you know about a system. Not disorder, and not decay.
Microstate, macrostate
The exact arrangement, and the coarse description it satisfies. Entropy is the bridge between them.
The past hypothesis
The assumption, needed to make the second law work, that the universe began in a state of extremely low entropy.
Order parameter
A quantity that is zero in one phase and non-zero in another — magnetisation, density difference, the condensate wavefunction. The measure of a broken symmetry.
Universality class
The set of utterly different systems that behave identically near a critical point. The reason a magnet and a fluid share numbers.
Landauer's limit
kT ln 2 of heat per bit erased. The price of forgetting, in joules.
Einselection
Environment-induced superselection: the environment picks out which states survive decoherence, and those are the ones that look classical.
Quantum Darwinism
Zurek's account of objectivity: a fact is objective when the environment holds many redundant copies of it.
Positive polarisation
In the Law of One material, the path of service to others, as against service to self. Primary esoteric text, used here as such.
Collapsed memory
The order's phrase for the single surviving history after decoherence — what the universe has actually kept.

A Closing Stance

The second law is the only law in physics that tells you which way the film is running, and the order has always suspected that a law like that is doing more than bookkeeping. That suspicion is not evidence. It is the reason to go on looking, and it is why the doctrine is written out here in full rather than hinted at — a thing stated plainly can be argued with, and a thing hinted at cannot.

If the teaching is right, some part of it will one day produce a number, and that number will be checkable. Until then it stands where it is: believed, useful, and filed as myth. Test everything kindly.
