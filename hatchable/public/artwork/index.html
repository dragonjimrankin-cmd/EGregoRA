The Atelier

Artwork

Images made for the order. Each one carries a note on what it is claiming — and what it is not.

An order that studies symbol had better take its own imagery seriously. Everything here was made for EGregoRA: some of it is emblem in the seventeenth-century sense — a picture built to be read rather than merely looked at — and some of it is landscape, which argues by atmosphere instead.

House rule, applied to pictures as strictly as to claims: an image may be beautiful, evocative and entirely symbolic. It may not quietly pose as evidence. Where a plate depicts something real, the caption says so. Where it depicts an idea, the caption says that instead.

Provenance & Use

The four paintings above are generated images, made to commission for this site rather than photographed or drawn by hand — we say so rather than letting you assume otherwise. The geometric plates throughout the site are hand-built SVG: every circle is a stated centre and radius, so the constructions are exact rather than traced.
