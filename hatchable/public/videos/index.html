A word before you go further

This site keeps nothing on your machine unless you let it. There are no advertising cookies, no trackers and no third-party analytics here — we sell nothing and measure no one. What we would like to store is small and local: your sign-in, so the oracle knows you on your return, and what you have asked it, so a conversation survives a reload. What an account is for.

Decline and everything still works — the site simply forgets you between visits, and you will be asked for your code each time. You can change your mind from the foot of any page.
